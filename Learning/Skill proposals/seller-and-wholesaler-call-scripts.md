# Seller and wholesaler call scripts

## Why it helps H.S HOLDINGS
Gives Tal and the partners a ready, ethical script for Indianapolis wholesaler meetings and for any direct-seller conversation, so motivation, timeline, and a real number are captured without over-offering.

## Suggested runner

If outbound seller calling becomes a regular function, a **new "Acquisitions Desk" bot** should own script versions, objection notes, and post-call logs. Grok Bot should only review or route, not run daily call ops.

## When to use
- Before a scheduled meeting or call with a wholesaler, seller, or agent who has a deal.
- When a hot lead is ready for a qualifying and close conversation.
- After a call, to check what was missed (motivation, timeline, decision maker, two contracts from a wholesaler, walkthrough window).

## Principles (from the training)
- Win-win, personal, time-sensitive, and persistent follow-up.
- Speak with the decision maker.
- The aim of the first seller contact is motivation + appointment, not a monologue about the buyer's story.
- Do not invent a precise offer on the call if the four MAO inputs are not in hand. Use `offer-mao-calculation`.
- This is a research and conversation aid. It is not legal advice and not a license to solicit in a way the state or TCPA forbids.

## Hot-lead seller script (from the Cycle 28 "Script for HOT Lead" document)
Adapt wording; keep the sequence. The source script assumes a prior assistant call and a PropStream-style research pass.

1. **Open.** Confirm you reached the seller, give your name, mention the property address and the prior contact, and ask if it is a bad time.
2. **Frame.** Ask permission to run about 7 minutes of questions. State that you are a real-estate investment company and do not buy every property, but that a realtor team can help if you are not the right buyer.
3. **Verify the asset.** Beds, baths, square feet, year built, HVAC age, roof age, electrical, plumbing, kitchen and bath updates versus "fair" condition. Correct the file if tax-record data is wrong.
4. **Motivation.** "Why are you looking to sell?" Listen. Do not skip this.
5. **Timeline.** If an offer made sense, would they be ready in about 7–10 days, 30+ days, or 60+ days?
6. **Comps and condition.** Talk through researched comps (source script: PropStream). Be factual. Tie value talk to the repairs they already described.
7. **Their number first.** Ask what they think the property is worth or the least they would take. If they refuse and demand your number, the source script says **do not give a single price — only a wide range**.
8. **Today-if-easy close question.** After you can cover customary closing costs, what is the best price they can do if it can be completed promptly?
9. **Pause.** "Let me speak with my partner." Ask if they would be ready to move forward today *if* that price works. Call back in 5–10 minutes (after an actual MAO check, not a stall).
10. **Close.** If the price is at or below MAO and partners agree, send a short agreement, review it together on the phone, confirm best phone and email, send to the title company, and book a property visit. A local partner ("boots") confirms or reschedules the visit.

Source-script tools named: PropStream for comps, HelloSign for a one-page agreement. Those brands may be replaced with a current e-sign and comp tool. Re-check the agreement with `purchase-contract-protections` before it is sent.

## Wholesaler conversation checklist
The class deal-day material treats wholesalers as a distinct path from FSBO and from listed deals.

- Ask for **both** documents: the **original purchase contract with the owner** and the **assignment agreement**. The assignment should state the wholesaler's fee; you step into the wholesaler's position and must see the underlying deal.
- Confirm who the decision maker is, inspection length, earnest money and whether it is refundable, closing date, occupancy, and access for inspection.
- Run the same four-number MAO on the **price you would actually pay** (contract price plus assignment fee, or the assignment purchase price).
- Do not rely on the wholesaler's ARV or rehab as final. Verify comps and get a contractor view.
- Stay courteous and fast. The training emphasizes follow-up and getting to contract only when the numbers work.

## After the call
Log: motivation in their words, timeline, decision maker, their asking number, any range you gave, inspection/access, documents promised, and the MAO decision (offer, pass, or more info).
