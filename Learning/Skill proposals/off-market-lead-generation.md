# Off-market lead generation process

## Why it helps H.S HOLDINGS
Gives a repeatable path to first-hand motivated sellers so the company is not limited to paying a wholesaler's full fee, while keeping 2020 cost and outreach tactics from being copied blindly.

## Suggested runner
**New dedicated bot ("Off-Market Desk")** is the better long-term runner: it would build and refresh lists, track funnel costs, and queue only screened hot leads. Until that bot exists, **Deal Inbox** can receive and tag incoming off-market leads, and **Zip Intel / Indianapolis Market Research** can help define list geography. Do not treat Grok Bot as the list engine.

## When to use
- Partners want a process for finding sellers off-market (probate, divorce, pre-foreclosure, vacant, out-of-state, inherited, high-equity, utility or court records).
- A marketing budget or vendor stack for lists, skip tracing, or a VA is being discussed.
- A lead source must be scored before anyone spends money or starts calling/texting.

Do not use this skill to place live calls or send texts. Use it to design the process, the checklist, and the compliance gates.

## Core idea
Off-market work is a bypass of the usual listing system: go directly to a motivated seller, confirm motivation, and book an appointment. Many attractive deals otherwise arrive only through wholesalers, at a fee. Direct-to-seller is a source, not a requirement to become a wholesaler.

## Funnel (from the 2020 training slides)
1. Pull a targeted list.
2. Skip-trace for current contact data.
3. Load the list into a CRM.
4. Outreach (calls and, only if currently lawful, other channels).
5. Manage and follow up (this step is mostly time).
6. Screen to a **hot lead**: motivation is real, and an appointment or a serious offer conversation is possible.

Goals of the first seller conversation: **motivation** and **appointment**.

## List sources taught in the material
Use as research starting points; verify what is still available and legal in the target county in the current year:

- PropStream (or a current equivalent list tool).
- County records.
- Water / electric utility records where legally available.
- Court records: eviction, divorce, probate.
- Other class sources for motivated sellers: inherited, out-of-state owners, vacant, pre-foreclosure, high-equity / free-and-clear, tired landlords, failed listings.

## 2020 cost snapshot — outdated, verify before budgeting
These figures are from April 2020 slides and must be treated as historical:

- Pull list: 97–300 dollars
- Skip trace: 250 dollars
- CRM upload: 197 dollars
- Cold calling: 500 dollars (described as 4 hours per day)
- Text blast: 100 dollars
- Follow-up: time

Do not reuse these numbers as a 2026 budget. Re-price every line. Re-check calling and texting law (including TCPA and state rules) before any blast or dialer plan. A "text blast for 100 dollars" is not a safe current default.

## Tools named in 2020 — verify before adopting
PropStream, YellowLetterHQ, Instantskiptracing, Podio, FreedomSoft, plus a VA source such as onlinejobs.ph. A specific 2020 VA vendor name and phone appeared on the slides; treat that contact as stale until re-verified, and do not reach out unless partners explicitly ask.

A local "boots" team still matters even with a list engine: realtor, contractor, attorney, inspector, title (and a property manager for rent checks).

## Operating rules
- Win-win, time-sensitive, follow up, personal touch.
- Speak with the decision maker.
- Know the exit (flip, rental/BRRRR, wholetail, or pass) before chasing a lead spending cycle.
- Volume without screening wastes skip-trace and call budget. Only "hot" leads should reach offer work (`offer-mao-calculation`) and contracts (`purchase-contract-protections`).
- Check current state rules on contacting sellers and on wholesaling/assignment *before* building a paid machine. Training comments about Michigan law do not apply to other states.

## Example defaults (H.S HOLDINGS)
- Primary geography: Indianapolis unless partners say otherwise. Atlanta is paused.
- Near term, prefer using this process as a design checklist and a filter for inbound / wholesaler / realtor leads. A full paid call/text operation is a later step, after a local team and a current compliance review.
- Hot leads still have to clear the company's MAO and cash-flow / flip-profit defaults before an offer goes out.

## Outdated or high-risk items
- All 2020 dollar costs and the text-blast tactic.
- Unverified 2020 vendor and VA contacts.
- Assuming a dialer or SMS vendor is compliant because a 2020 course used one.

### 2026 owner-identification tools (no skip tracing)
Public records give owner name + mailing address, not phone. A skip-traced number gives no consent for calls/texts → **direct mail is the safe channel**.

| Tool | What you get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Marion County assessor cards / MapIndy | owner, address, parcel | free |
| Marion recorder, tax-sale reports, mycase | motivation (estate, eviction, divorce, tax debt) | basic free; Tapestry $6.95/search |
| PropertyRadar | owner, mailing address, absentee, equity, distress, mail | $119/mo, postcard 66¢ |
| PropStream | ownership, liens, foreclosures, 165 filters, mail | sub price not on official page; postcard 57¢ |
| DealMachine | driving for dollars, AI postcards | Basic ~$119/mo (unverified), postcard 75¢ |
| RealEstateAPI / ATTOM / Regrid | owner-data API | from $599/mo; ATTOM/Regrid unpublished |

TCPA exposure for calls, texts and AI voice: **$500–1,500 per violation**.
