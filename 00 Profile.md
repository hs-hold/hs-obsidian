## Deal criteria
- **Primary strategy is BRRRR**; flip is a secondary alternative. The pass/fail call and the max offer follow the BRRRR target (owner, 2026-11-12).
- Flip: profit ≥ $30k **and** ROI ≥ 15%, with price ≥ 15% below ARV including selling costs. A listing can pass the pre-filter at asking or after a discount of up to about 15–25% off asking (owner, 2026-11-10). Base flip hold is 6 months, with 12 months as a stress case (owner, 2026-11-12).
- BRRRR: equity ≥ 15% **and** ≥ $30k, cash left in the deal after a 65% LTV refi ≤ $60k, cash flow ≥ $250/mo.
- Rental screen: near the 1% rule (monthly rent vs purchase + rehab).
- Indianapolis purchase budget is larger than $150k; evaluate deals up to about $300k if returns still meet criteria, including the $250/mo BRRRR cash-flow target (owner, 2026-11-16). In core Indy zips, BRRRR at market rents often ceilings well below $300k.
- Beds 2–5 are acceptable (5BR added 2026-11-13).
- In BRRRR search, weight higher rent favorably and flag "works with discount" deals where a modest price cut makes the $250/mo target pass with max cash left $60k; rate the top deal 1–10 (owner, 2026-11-15).
- **Trial criteria Nov 18–24, 2026** (owner, 2026-11-17 22:27): 65% LTV refi (30y, 7.5%), up to $60k own cash left in, flip profit ≈ $20–25k+, cash flow ≥ $250/mo self-managed, 12% reserves; prefer ARV $200–300k, lower ARV OK in a very good area if numbers work. Plan A/B/C for passes; also mark old BRRRR pass/fail. Compare on 2026-11-25 10:00 and the owner picks.
- **MAO in every analysis** (owner, 2026-11-17): highest price at which the deal still passes, next to asking, with the % off it needs. No extra conservatism layers.

## Analysis rules
- Verified data only — no default or estimated ARV or rent. Every number gets a source.
- ARV from closed comps, last 6 months, within half a mile, same type, filtered by condition; MEDIAN of adjusted prices. Comp filters and dollar adjustments: `/home/box/shared/agent-rules/comp-adjustments.md` (owner-approved 2026-11-09). Indianapolis-specific research: `/home/box/shared/indy-arv-adjustments.md`.
- Comps must never be taken from across a highway; a highway is a hard barrier, including for the most relevant comps (owner, 2026-11-11).
- Real time-to-sell comes from listing history, including take-downs and relists — not only the "Listed for sale" date. If a property left the market and came back at a much higher price, treat it as likely renovated and count time-to-sell only from the latest relist (owner, 2026-11-11).
- Before choosing comps, read the zip note in [[Atlanta]] / [[Indianapolis]] (source files also live at `/home/box/shared/zip-intel/<zip>.md`). Owner notes in those files override research.
- Average up to three Zillow school ratings (two if only two exist). Owner prefers 4 or higher; alert clearly below 4.
- Flag if the owner of record is a company rather than an individual.
- Tell the owner whenever Deal Analysis data is not fully certain or is hard to obtain, so he can compromise and still get the data (owner, 2026-11-13).
- For every high-potential deal, examine what would make it work (price, rehab, rent, financing, expenses, or strategy change) — the "what makes it work" / levers card (owner approved the rules 2026-11-12; site merge/deploy needed separate approval).
- The deal journal (compare actual rent and expenses after a purchase against our assumptions) is activated **only** when the owner explicitly says he bought a property. Until then, no background checks, runs, or builds related to it (owner, 2026-11-15).
- **Deep micro-location check (owner, 2026-11-17):** when a deal shows high potential, [[Deal Analysis]] investigates the micro-area in depth before the verdict: which side of the highway or creek it is on, comps from the same pocket, noise, flood, crime, vacant properties, street quality, and why nearby prices are low. The findings go into the conclusion. (Triggered by [[1733 Olive St]].)

## Deal-search pipeline
1. Site code (`funnel-run-next-zip`) pulls **one zip at a time** (the least recently pulled) with minimal RapidAPI calls. Each zip at most ~once a week.
2. Never call RapidAPI directly from an agent. Never pull many zips in a burst (on 2026-11-12, 42 calls on 20 zips jammed the DB and needed a restart).
3. Pre-filter in code (no tokens). Passing deals go to Deal Analysis (max ~5 per run) for a second filter and full analysis, then write-back to the site.
4. If a deal was analyzed in the last 6 months and only the price changed: recalc price-dependent figures only — no new API/AI comps work.
5. Daily automatic search: 8:00 Asia/Jerusalem (owner, 2026-11-11). Report interesting deals in Hebrew with the site link and the usage the run took.
6. Morning owner update at 9:30 Asia/Jerusalem; no notifications before then (owner, 2026-11-10). Hold earlier results for that update.
