- **DeKalb multifamily 4+ CCC** applies in unincorporated areas; cities may have own occupational-tax / inspection overlays — verify.
- **STR:** unincorporated DeKalb STR license ($175) since May 20, 2026; city STR rules may differ — check Lithonia/Stonecrest before listing.
