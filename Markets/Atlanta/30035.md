## Permits & code enforcement
- **Jurisdiction:** DeKalb County Planning & Sustainability (unincorporated). Online ePermits / ProjectDox; residential renovations, additions, trades via portal. County adopted 2024 ICC codes effective Jan 1, 2026.
- **Timelines:** Official site says plan-review time varies by completeness; practitioner guides cite roughly **2–4 weeks** for typical residential renovations when submittals are clean (not a hard SLA).
- **CO:** Certificate of Occupancy after final inspections via DeKalb Development Services.
- **Code compliance:** DeKalb Code Compliance investigates complaints; initial inspections typically within **3 business days**. No general single-family rental registry (state-blocked — see below). Multifamily (4+ units) owners in unincorporated DeKalb must file annual **Code Compliance Certificate** with business occupation tax; inspect ≥20% of units/year, every unit ≤5 years.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/planning-and-sustainability/permits-plan-review-and-inspections ; https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/code-compliance ; https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/code-compliance/apartment-complex-registrations

## City attitude to investors
- Georgia **O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30(b)** bars local governments from requiring **residential rental property registration** and limits rental inspections to probable cause (plain-view conditions can support PC). Effective text includes 2025 HB 399 amendment.
- DeKalb officials have publicly pushed for state authority to create a broader rental/owner registry after vacant-house fire coverage (WSB-TV) — signal of **tighter landlord accountability politics**, not an enacted SFR registry.
- No local ban on small LLC single-family purchases found. Federal mega-investor home-purchase limits (350+ SFR) take effect Jan 7, 2027 — largely irrelevant to a small self-managed LLC.
- **STR (unincorporated DeKalb):** license required since **May 20, 2026**; $175/year + processing; apply before advertising.
- **Sources:** https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-36/provisions-applicable-to-counties-and-municipal-corporations/chapter-74/article-2/section-36-74-30/ ; https://engagedekalb.dekalbcountyga.gov/short-term-rental ; https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/dekalb/dekalb-county-pushing-statewide-registry-homes-disrepair/6FNIFS62QJFWPPLCM3GI5KNWMM/

## Transit & access
- Auto-oriented south DeKalb; I-20 / I-285 access.
- MARTA **rail is not walkable** for most of the zip — nearest Blue Line options are Indian Creek / Kensington / Avondale depending on address; rely on bus + park-and-ride. Job access leans toward downtown Atlanta, airport corridor, and DeKalb employment nodes by car.
- **Source:** MARTA system maps / local real-estate area guides.

## Taxes
- **2026 unincorporated DeKalb county millage:** **21.310** mills (up 0.5 from 20.810 in 2025). County presentation: ~$69/year more for average ~$364K FMV home from the half-mill hike.
- Plus DeKalb County School District millage (confirm current school millage on tax bill — not restated here without a 2026 school-board cite in hand).
- Assessment: Georgia standard 40% assessment ratio for county bills. EHOST homestead relief reduced in 2026 vs 2025 for many owners — **investor (non-homestead) properties do not get homestead/EHOST shelter**, so reassessment risk bites harder after purchase/rehab.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026%20Millage%20Rates%20Public%20Hearing%20Presentation%202026.06.23%201.pdf ; https://www.decaturish.com/news/dekalb/dekalb-county-commissioners-vote-to-raise-property-taxes/article_8ce10501-6908-4865-b919-d06c52723ec0.html
