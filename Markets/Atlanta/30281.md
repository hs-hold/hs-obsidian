## Area name
**Stockbridge / unincorporated Henry County.** PlainZIP: only ~**16%** of land inside **City of Stockbridge**; ~**84%** outside Census places (mostly **unincorporated Henry**). Confirm city limits — permits, STR, and city millage (3.77) apply only inside the city.

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**2 / 5** — Partner-rated core BRRRR, and liquidity is strong (~207 sales/3 mo), but **1% needs ~47% under ZHVI** on SAFMR (worse on ZORI), HOA rental caps are common in Henry/Stockbridge product, and high schools remain below-average. Better as selective distress BRRRR than as a volume play.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **Unincorporated Henry:** SagesGov permitting portal; residential reno/addition checklists; state minimum codes with GA amendments effective **Jan 1, 2026**.
- **City of Stockbridge:** separate SagesGov portal (stockbridge-ga); Community Development enforces UDC; stop-work / municipal court path for violations.
- **LTR registration:** none found for ordinary SFR (O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30).
- **STR:** Henry County occupational tax + local emergency contact ≤20 miles + hotel/motel excise. **City of Stockbridge** has its own STR ordinance (registration, standards, 8% tax; AJC: stays generally 3–14 nights under the approved rules) — confirm current text before any STR plan. Not the BRRRR hold thesis.
- **Sources:** sagesgov.com/henrycounty-ga; sagesgov.com/stockbridge-ga; AJC Stockbridge STR; Henry STR getting-started; Municode Stockbridge.

## City attitude to investors
- Stockbridge previously approved a **moratorium on applications for single-family rental / built-to-rent subdivisions** (AJC / Henry County Times, 2021) — clear political resistance to institutional rental communities.
- HOA registration ordinance exists at city level (Municode references). Small LLC one-off BRRRR still legal; expect scrutiny on large rental clusters.
- No blanket ban on buying individual SFH for long-term rent found.

## Crime
- **Stockbridge PD** (city): Police Census / property-crime trend sources report 2025 totals on the order of ~157 violent / ~656 property (agency-level — verify). 2026 YTD partials lower as expected mid-year.
- **Henry County PD** covers unincorporated majority of the zip — countywide aggregates only; use CrimeMapping / open records per address.
- Zip-level official series **not found**. Secondary city “safety grade” sites exist — label secondary.
- **Sources:** Police Census Stockbridge/Henry; propertycrimetrends.com (secondary).

## Transit & access
- Auto-first; I-75 / Hudson Bridge / Eagles Landing retail corridor. MARTA essentially absent; Xpress/commuter options limited. Job access: Henry employment, logistics, and north to airport/downtown by I-75 (often 30–45+ min).

## Taxes
- **Unincorporated Henry:** calculator example uses combined **39.533** mills on 40% FMV (includes schools). School M&O **20.000** mills (FY27 board reporting). County unincorporated portion cited ~**15.733** mills in VoteGA-style tables.
- **City of Stockbridge:** city millage **3.77** (2026 council/budget discussion) **plus** reduced county line for city parcels (VoteGA cites Stockbridge county rate ~**10.733**) + school 20.000 — **verify on tax bill**.
- Investor non-homestead; reassessment risk after reno.
- **Sources:** henrycountytax.com; Henry Herald FY27 millage; VoteGA Henry; Stockbridge FY2026 budget discussion (3.77 mills).

## HOA
- **High HOA prevalence** relative to Clayton BRRRR zips — 1990s–2020s subdivisions, townhomes, gated products. Examples in the Stockbridge/Henry market include **rental percentage caps** (e.g. reported 5% rental cap on some approved townhome projects). **Underwrite CC&Rs before offer** — a lease ban or low rental cap kills BRRRR.
- City has taken interest in HOA registration historically.
- **Sources:** Henry GA News townhome coverage; AJC BTR moratorium; local covenant practice.

## Insurance
- Scale to ~$250–300K+ dwellings; Henry/Clayton averages ~$2.3–2.5K class. Flood: FEMA address check (local creeks). Georgia premium inflation applies.

## Other pros/cons
**Market / rent (estimates — AMR canvas: ZHVI/ZORI Sep 2026, Redfin Jul–Sep 2026, HUD FY2027 SAFMR):**
- ZHVI 3BR ~**$258,048**; Redfin median ~**$294,913**; ZORI ~**$1,685**; SAFMR 3BR **$1,890**; ~**38%** renters; DOM ~**57**; **~207** sales/3 mo (most liquid of the three); YoY **−2.7%**; median year ~**1995**.
- **Rent vs SAFMR:** ZORI **$1,685 vs SAFMR $1,890 → ZORI ≈ 10.8% below SAFMR** (SAFMR/ZORI ≈ 1.12). Prefer ZORI/comps for non-HCV deals.

**1% rule math (estimate):** Assumptions — $49k rehab, 2% buy close, rent = SAFMR 3BR.
- Need T ≤ $189,000 → purchase ≤ ~$137,255 → **discount ≈ 47% under ZHVI**.
- On ZORI $1,685 → **discount ≈ 55% under ZHVI**. Estimates. Harder 1% path than 30274/30273.

**Flip test (estimate):** For both $30k profit and 15% ROI, 15% binds first → T ≤ ~$206,438 → purchase ≤ ~$154,351 → **~40% under ZHVI**. At 30% off, model profit only ~$4k / ROI ~2%. **Flip: fail** without deep discount (at ~40% off, both hurdles roughly clear as estimates).

**Pros:** Liquidity; Eagles Landing retail amenity; slightly stronger HS option (Woodland) than Riverdale Drew.  
**Cons:** Expensive basis for BRRRR math; HOA lease caps; city politics vs rental subdivisions; ZORI below SAFMR.
