**3 / 5** — Classic south DeKalb rental-demand zip with mid entry (~$251k) and DeKalb rules that are known quantities; owner-occupied still ~68% (thinner renters than Forest Park/Morrow), schools weak, and crime lacks a clean zip primary source.
