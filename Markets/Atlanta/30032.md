## Area name
Postal name Decatur; **not** City of Decatur proper (that is mainly 30030) and **not** City Schools of Decatur. Entirely DeKalb County. Unincorporated. By Census place (land share): **Candler-McAfee CDP ~51%**, **Belvedere Park CDP ~31%**, ~18% outside any Census place. Small fringes toward Atlanta / Avondale Estates exist at the edges — always confirm the parcel is unincorporated DeKalb, not a city. ~43k people (ACS 2020–2024). Borders 30002, 30030, 30317, 30034, 30035, 30316, 30021, 30083. Typical extents: **Memorial Dr** on the north, **I-20** on the south, **I-285** on the east; **Candler Rd (GA-155)** and **Columbia Dr** are the main north–south spines; **Glenwood Rd** is the practical Belvedere / Candler-McAfee divide.

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3 / 5** — Mid-century ranch inventory that can work for selective flips (Deal Analysis, Nov 2026, had two passing flips in Belvedere Park) and for BRRRR with street screening. DeKalb rules are a known quantity and Georgia bars a local SFR rental registry. Offsets that keep it mid-tier: weak DeKalb zoned-school ratings, high ACS vacancy, soft 2025–26 values (FHFA −4.2% in 2025; ZHVI −4.2% YoY to Sep 2026), 1950s–70s sewer-lateral risk (Orangeburg / clay / cast iron), no official zip-level crime series, and rent that clears the 1% rule only at a deep discount to typical value. Belvedere Park, especially west toward East Lake and north toward Decatur, is the stronger flip pocket; do not zip-average it with I-20 / Candler commercial frontage or deep Candler-McAfee.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **Jurisdiction:** Almost entirely **unincorporated DeKalb** — Planning & Sustainability, **e-Permits + ProjectDox**. County adopted 2024 ICC codes effective Jan 1, 2026. Certificate of Occupancy after final inspections via DeKalb Development Services.
- **Timelines:** Official site says plan-review time varies by completeness; practitioner guides cite roughly **2–4 weeks** for a typical residential renovation when submittals are clean (not a hard SLA). Budget longer if structural, zoning, or watershed issues appear.
- **Code compliance:** DeKalb Code Compliance, 404-687-3700; complaint-driven, initial inspections typically within **3 business days**. Property maintenance, junk vehicles, and overgrowth enforcement are common on older south/east DeKalb stock — budget for exterior compliance.
- **Multifamily 4+ units** in unincorporated DeKalb: annual Code Compliance Certificate with occupation tax; inspect ≥20% of units/year, every unit ≤5 years. **No general SFR rental registry** (state-blocked — see below).
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/planning-and-sustainability/permits-plan-review-and-inspections ; https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/code-compliance ; https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/code-compliance/apartment-complex-registrations

## City attitude to investors
- Georgia **O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30(b)** bars local governments from requiring **residential rental property registration** and limits rental inspections to probable cause (plain-view conditions can support PC), including the 2025 HB 399 amendment.
- DeKalb officials have publicly pushed for state authority to create a broader rental/owner registry after vacant-house fire coverage (WSB-TV) — a signal of **tighter landlord-accountability politics**, not an enacted SFR registry.
- Unincorporated DeKalb: business license / occupation tax for rental operations; **STR license** required since **May 20, 2026** ($175/yr + processing) for stays ≤30 days — not a long-term BRRRR issue but do not advertise short-term without it.
- No zip-specific anti-investor ordinance, flip moratorium, or small-LLC purchase ban found (Nov 12, 2026). DeKalb’s Housing Office / affordability focus is worth watching for future rules.
- **Sources:** https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-36/provisions-applicable-to-counties-and-municipal-corporations/chapter-74/article-2/section-36-74-30/ ; https://engagedekalb.dekalbcountyga.gov/short-term-rental ; https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/dekalb/dekalb-county-pushing-statewide-registry-homes-disrepair/6FNIFS62QJFWPPLCM3GI5KNWMM/

## Transit & access
- Auto-oriented but **better job/intown access than 30058 / deeper east DeKalb**. I-20 and I-285 are close; Candler Rd runs north into Decatur; Columbia Dr and Glenwood Rd connect toward East Atlanta and East Lake.
- **No MARTA rail station inside the zip.** Kensington, Avondale, and East Lake stations are a short drive depending on address (Belvedere west/north is closest to East Lake / Avondale; eastern tracts lean Kensington / Indian Creek). Bus service along Memorial, Candler, Columbia, and Glenwood. MARTA has studied South DeKalb / I-20 East high-capacity transit and a Candler Rd arterial-rapid-transit concept (planning/design, not open service as of 2026).
- ACS average commute ~32 minutes (knowground / ACS 2020–2024). Downtown, Decatur, Emory/CDC, and intown jobs are reachable by car; plan a rush-hour drive.
- **Sources:** MARTA project dashboard / system maps; Elements Atlanta Belvedere guide; https://www.knowground.com/zip/30032

## HOA

## Other pros/cons
**Pros**
- Mid-century brick-ranch stock close to Decatur, Avondale Estates, and East Lake — a genuine flip location premium versus deeper south/east DeKalb, especially in Belvedere Park.
- Deal Analysis (Nov 2026) underwrote **two passing flips** in this zip, both in Belvedere Park CDP (see Sub-areas).
- Entry values below the DeKalb middle: ZHVI typical home **$233,457** (Sep 2026, −4.2% YoY); ACS owner-estimated median value $273,600. Redfin 3-month median **sale** (ending Oct 2026) **$284,854** / **$190 per sqft**, 84 DOM, sale-to-list 96.5% — somewhat competitive, with 45.8% of listings taking a price cut.
- Renter share of occupied units ~**43–44%** (ACS) — thicker rental demand than many owner-heavy south DeKalb zips. HUD FY2027 SAFMR (effective Oct 1, 2026): 2BR **$1,490**, 3BR **$1,780**. ZORI typical rent **$1,688** (Sep 2026).
- FHFA ZIP HPI: **+167%** 2015–25 and **+38%** 2020–25 (long-run gain), though **−4.2%** in 2025.
- Single-county, unincorporated underwriting (simpler than 30058’s city split).

**Cons**
- **1% rule is tight.** 1% of ZHVI ≈ $2,335/mo; ZORI $1,688 is ~0.72% of typical value, and even SAFMR 3BR $1,780 is ~0.76%. A 1% path on ZORI implies all-in near ~$169k — a deep discount to typical value, before treating rehab as extra.
- ACS vacancy is **high**: PlainZIP/ACS 21,045 units and 16,285 households imply ~**23%** vacant (all-vacant, not just rentals); BestNeighborhood block-group rollup **19.9%**. Underwrite lease-up, not zip-average occupancy.
- Zoned **DeKalb County Schools** (not City Schools of Decatur) — ratings generally weak (see Sub-areas). Family ARV is school-sensitive; do not use 30030 Decatur-city comps.
- Soft near-term collateral (FHFA 2025 and ZHVI YoY both −4.2%); 7.2 months of supply in Lotvox/Redfin reading through Sep 2026.
- Orangeburg / clay / cast-iron laterals on 1950s–70s stock — scope every pre-1980 house (see Sub-areas).
- Crime: no official zip series; secondary models are poor; street variance is the underwriting fact.
- Median HH income $62,765; poverty 16.7%; unemployment 9.3% (ACS 2020–2024). Bachelor’s+ 30.4%.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 12, 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** Entirely DeKalb; almost all **unincorporated**. Not City of Decatur. Do not use 30030 City of Decatur or City Schools of Decatur sales as comps. Confirm any edge parcel that might touch Atlanta or Avondale Estates city limits. (PlainZIP; Census places.)
- **Belvedere Park CDP (~31% of land; north of Glenwood Rd, south of Memorial Dr, west of I-285):** 1950s planned brick-ranch community. **Favor for flips** when the subject is a similar ranch on an interior street. Closer to Decatur / Avondale / East Lake; Kensington–Avondale–East Lake MARTA by a short drive. **Deal Analysis passing flips (Nov 2026), both in Belvedere Park CDP, Census tract 231.07 (Census geocoder, Nov 12, 2026):**
  - **1564 Columbia Dr** — Belvedere Park along the **Columbia Dr** spine (33.744, −84.259). Treat as **Columbia Dr / central Belvedere** — arterial-adjacent; do not blindly use quiet cul-de-sac comps without a location adjustment, and do not pull south-of-Glenwood Candler-McAfee sales as straight comps.
  - **3425 Longleaf Dr** — **Pinehill Estates**, still Belvedere Park CDP, slightly east (33.747, −84.249), near Peachcrest Rd / the I-285 side. Same CDP, different micro-pocket than Columbia Dr; prefer Pinehill / eastern Belvedere comps first.
- **Candler-McAfee CDP (~51%; generally south of Glenwood Rd / Glenwood Ave, north of I-20, west of I-285):** Separate sub-market — more 1950s–70s south-DeKalb stock, closer to I-20. **Do not treat a Candler-McAfee sale as a straight Belvedere Park comp** (or vice versa). Wikipedia geography: bounded by Glenwood Avenue, 2nd Avenue, East Lake, East Atlanta, I-20, I-285. (Wikipedia Candler-McAfee; PlainZIP.)
- **Hard edges:** **I-20** (south), **I-285** (east), **Memorial Dr** (north), **Glenwood Rd** (Belvedere vs Candler-McAfee). **Candler Rd** and **Columbia Dr** are comparability breaks — prefer the same side and similar distance to the interstate. Covington Hwy is more of a 30035/east edge issue; do not pull those sales unadjusted. West toward **East Lake (30317)** and north toward **Decatur (30030)** are different buyer pools and school systems.
- **Orangeburg / sewer laterals (high relevance here):** Median year built **~1969**; Belvedere was platted in the 1950s. That is the core era for **Orangeburg** (bituminous fiber, commonly used ~1940s–early 1970s), plus **vitrified clay** and **cast iron**. DeKalb has **no public address inventory** of Orangeburg laterals — presence is house-specific, sometimes street-clustered, sometimes isolated. Private laterals are the **owner’s responsibility**; DeKalb Watershed will notify owners when smoke-testing finds a defect. **Practical rule:** CCTV sewer-scope every pre-1980 (and especially 1950–1975) house during due diligence, early enough to get a plumber’s written bid. Failed Orangeburg is often a **replace**, not a simple liner, if the pipe is ovalized or crushed. Industry inspection write-ups cite driveway-excavation replacements often in the **~$7,500–$18,000+** range (vendor estimate, not a bid). Ask the inspector whether they will actually identify Orangeburg (InterNACHI standards do not require it). DeKalb plumbing permit required for a new private sewer connection. (Foresight Home Inspections DeKalb/Decatur sewer alert; The Agency Atlanta sewer-scope guide, Oct 2026; DeKalb sewer-tap / OSARP materials.)
- **Schools (DeKalb County School District — verify the DCSD attendance locator per address; zones cut the zip):**
  - **Belvedere Park** historically maps toward **Peachcrest ES** (in the CDP) / Snapfinger (outside), **Mary McLeod Bethune** or Columbia middle, and **Towers HS** (in the CDP) or Columbia HS (outside). (Wikipedia Belvedere Park, citing DCSD 2016–17 maps — **re-verify; maps age**.)
  - **Candler-McAfee** historically maps toward Columbia, Kelley Lake, McNair, Snapfinger, and Toney elementaries; McNair or Columbia middle; **Columbia HS** or **McNair HS**. (Wikipedia Candler-McAfee.)
  - Ratings are generally **weak** on GreatSchools / proficiency: examples cited in 2026 directories include Towers HS **1/10**, Columbia HS **2/10**, Peachcrest ES **2/10**; NeighborScore (EDFacts SY 2021–22, older) shows very low math/reading proficiency at Towers, Columbia HS, and Peachcrest. **Wadsworth Magnet** (GreatSchools **9/10** in some listings) and City of Decatur schools are **not** the default zoned option for a 30032 address. School-zone mismatch is a primary non-comparability flag for family ARV. (GreatSchools; neighborscore.app/georgia/decatur/30032/schools; DCSD locator.)
- **Investor / SFR-fund saturation:** Metro context only — **no reliable zip-level ownership share found for 30032** (Nov 12, 2026). AJC reporting: investors own about **30% of metro Atlanta single-family rental homes**. Invitation Homes, Progress Residential, and other institutional landlords are large metro-wide owners (Invitation Homes on the order of ~7,800 metro Atlanta homes in 2024 Senate/letter figures). A 2023 regional housing-framework cite put DeKalb investor-owned SFRs at a **much smaller share of all rental units (~8%)** — different denominator, not a 30032 figure. Georgia Tech work (Elora Raymond and others) documents corporate-landlord purchasing in metro Atlanta but not a clean 30032 share. **Parcel check:** Who Owns Atlanta (DeKalb coverage) and the tax card. High ACS vacancy and a ~43% renter share mean small investors are already active; do not assume a street is “not investor-heavy.” (AJC “investors now own 30%”; Who-Owns-Atlanta; Georgia Tech corporate-landlord paper.)
- **Non-comparable:** Commercial Candler / Memorial / Columbia frontage vs interior yards; I-20 noise lots; East Lake / City of Decatur / 30316 Village comps; unfinished flips vs permitted renovated; floodplain or creek lots vs Zone X; magnet-school or City of Decatur school comps.
