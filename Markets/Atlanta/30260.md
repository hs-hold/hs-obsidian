## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3.5 / 5** — Cheaper regulatory load than Forest Park (no citywide long-term rental inspection program found), lower city millage (~7.5), solid renter share (~53%), and cheapish entry (~$206k). ## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Morrow** Planning & Economic Development for city parcels: building, demo, MEP, LDP, signs via **Community Core** online portal; applications processed Mon–Thu. Contact Zoning Admin (Marti Tracy) for zoning/permits/code questions.
- Unincorporated Clayton / Lake City: use that jurisdiction’s building department — confirm address.
- Code Enforcement Division sits under Morrow PD; proactive ordinance enforcement (street/yard parking bans, etc.). Report: codeenforcement@morrowga.gov / 470-696-5287.
- Difficulty: moderate; smaller city staff can mean slower turnaround than metro giants — plan buffer.

## City attitude to investors
- **No dedicated long-term rental registration/inspection ordinance found** for City of Morrow (Nov 9, 2026 search) — contrast with Forest Park. Landlords typically need **occupational tax certificate** if operating as a business; confirm with Planning/Finance.
- **STR:** no city-specific STR ordinance found; state tax + Morrow occupational tax + hotel/motel tax registration typically apply. Clayton County STR rules (owner-occupied only) apply to **unincorporated** Clayton, not automatically to Morrow city — verify.
- City marketing emphasizes business attraction via Planning & Economic Development — generally open to investment, with standard code enforcement.
