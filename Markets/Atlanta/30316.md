## Area name
Postal name Atlanta. **Split jurisdiction — critical:**
- County: DeKalb ~84% of land, Fulton ~16% (Census 2020).
- Places: City of Atlanta ~29%, Gresham Park CDP ~21%, **~50% outside any Census place** (unincorporated DeKalb / other).
- Neighborhoods commonly associated: East Atlanta Village, Ormewood, parts of Glenwood, Bouldercrest, Gresham Park.
- ~35k people; median HH income ~$103k (ACS 2020–2024) — highest of this research batch.
- **Always verify city limits + county on the parcel** (Atlanta GIS city-limits layer vs DeKalb unincorporated). Rules, taxes, schools, and police all change.

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3.5 / 5** — Genuine gentrification / appreciation story (FHFA +40% 2020–25; median value ~$443k), improving citywide crime trend, and workable Atlanta permit timelines — but jurisdiction split, higher capital, APS/DeKalb school variance, and AMR ROI only borderline (~13%). Good flip zip when the address is clearly City of Atlanta with clean comps; discount unincorporated/Gresham Park parcels differently.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Atlanta parcels:** Office of Buildings via **Accela Citizen Access**. Process: intake (~48 hrs) → plan review (often ~10–14 days initial) → revisions → fees → issue → inspections → CO/CC.
  - 2026 median application-to-issuance ~**22 days** overall (PermitPager summary) — faster than older lore of multi-month waits for simple rehabs; structural/zoning/arborist/fire can still push **4–12 weeks**.
  - Typical flip rehab budget **4–8 weeks** for permit cycle (industry guides).
- **Unincorporated DeKalb parcels:** DeKalb e-Permits + ProjectDox (~2–4 weeks typical rehab review).
- Code enforcement: Atlanta 311 / Zoning Enforcement for city; DeKalb Code Compliance for unincorporated. East Atlanta civic groups (EACA) actively flag vacant/nuisance properties — expect neighbor eyes on messy job sites.

## City attitude to investors
- City of Atlanta: growth + housing production pressure; active STR regulation (**$150/yr STR license**, primary residence + one additional unit framework historically; 2026 proposals to expand STR office/registry — watch ordinance 26-O-1084 status). **Long-term rentals: no general city rental registry found**; occupational tax certificate if operating as business; CO after permitted work.
- Gentrification politics: EACA / District 5 discussions note Black population decline amid new housing — community sensitivity to speculative flips exists; not a legal ban.
- Unincorporated DeKalb: standard county occupation tax / 4+ unit apartment registration / STR license.
- No flip moratorium found (Nov 9, 2026). Permit activity continues (e.g. multifamily/renovation permits in 30316 tracked by permit aggregators).

## Crime
- City portions: **Atlanta PD Zone 6**.
- Citywide APD: crime down ~7% in 2025 and ~13% YoY as of mid-Oct 2026 (APD / WSB reporting) — **citywide, not zip-specific**.
- Zip aggregator CrimeAtlanta.com: **1,648** reported incidents for 30316 (217 violent / 1,431 property), grade **B-**, theft most common — **secondary aggregator; methodology opaque; use as soft signal**.
- EACA minutes (2025–2026): ongoing concern about shootings / business break-ins even as trends improve — local qualitative.
- Unincorporated/Gresham Park: DeKalb PD — separate stats.
- **Best practice:** APD open crime downloads + DeKalb Part 1 GIS for the exact block.

## Transit & access
- East Atlanta Village walkable retail/food; Moreland Ave / Glenwood corridor.
- MARTA: bus-rich; nearest rail often **Inman Park/Reynoldstown** or East Lake (~2 miles depending on address) — better transit than Smyrna/Clayton BRRRR zips but still often car-plus.
- Strong intown job access (downtown, Midtown, BeltLine-adjacent pockets where applicable).

## Taxes
- **City of Atlanta 2026 (adopted):** city general 9.52 + bonds 0.85 + parks 1.00 + **APS 20.300** mills; plus **DeKalb special tax district 0.450** for Atlanta-in-DeKalb parcels; BeltLine/Stitch SSD **+2.00** each where mapped (Atlanta Live News / council ordinance reporting).
- County layer: DeKalb or Fulton depending on parcel (Fulton general ~8.87 mills reported for 2026).
- Unincorporated DeKalb (no Atlanta): ~21.310 county + 22.78 DeKalb Schools ≈ 44.09 mills — **different school system (DeKalb vs APS)** which changes buyer pool for flips.
- Assessed at 40% FMV. Rising assessments are a real holding-cost risk if flip stalls; digest growth has outpaced millage cuts.

## HOA
- Mostly historic SFR / bungalows without HOA in East Atlanta Village core; more HOA/townhome product toward newer infill. Confirm per property. Intown HOAs can be strict on exterior during rehab.

## Insurance
- Metro Atlanta HO averages often cited ~$2.3k–$3.4k depending on source/year; older intown stock (knob-and-tube, roof age, proximity claims) can be hard-to-place — **get landlord/vacant-rehab quotes before closing**.
- Flood: FEMA MSC by address; some low-lying Glenwood/Intrenchment Creek areas need care. Standard HO excludes flood.

## Other pros/cons
**Pros (flip emphasis)**
- Median home value ~$443k; median gross rent ~$1,717; HH income ~$103k; bachelor’s+ 57% — strong end-buyer demographics.
- Gentrifying / established intown brand (East Atlanta) supports ARV; AMR: Redfin ARV clears $30k profit, ROI ~13% at modeled discount.
- Atlanta permit times improved vs historical reputation for simple rehabs.
- FHFA +108% 2015–25 / +2.1% in 2025 — still positive vs several south zips that fell in 2025.

**Cons**
- Jurisdiction roulette (Atlanta vs unincorporated DeKalb vs Gresham Park) — wrong assumption = wrong taxes/schools/permits.
- Capital intensive; ROI thinner than primary flip targets (30030/30317).
- Schools: APS for city addresses (variable; many East Atlanta zoned schools mid GreatSchools); DeKalb Schools for unincorporated — buyer sensitivity high for family ARV.
- Crime still a marketing objection for some buyers despite citywide improvement.
- Community pushback risk on obvious speculative flips / STRs.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Jurisdiction (hard comps rule):** City of **Atlanta** (~29% of land) vs **Gresham Park CDP** (~21%) vs **unincorporated DeKalb** (~50%); also DeKalb vs Fulton county line. City of Atlanta = APS + Atlanta permits/taxes; unincorporated DeKalb = DeKalb Schools + DeKalb permits. **Never treat a Gresham Park / unincorporated sale as a straight East Atlanta Village comp.** Use Atlanta GIS city-limits layer. (PlainZIP; atlantalivenews East Atlanta; Atlanta GIS.)
- **Named intown pockets (City of Atlanta side — prefer same pocket):** **East Atlanta Village** (Flat Shoals / Glenwood / Moreland core); **Ormewood Park** (west toward BeltLine / Grant Park); **East Atlanta** broader south of I-20; parts of Glenwood, Brownwood. **I-20** is a hard north barrier for many buyers — north-of-I-20 (e.g. toward Reynoldstown/Inman Park in other zips) ≠ south-of-I-20 EAV comps. (Wikipedia East Atlanta; Elements Ormewood.)
- **Gresham Park / Bouldercrest:** Separate market — more suburban DeKalb stock; Bouldercrest Rd often marks the transition. Do not pull Village bungalow comps for Gresham Park ranches.
- **Schools:** City addresses → **APS** (commonly Burgess-Peterson / MLK MS / Maynard Jackson HS paths — **confirm APS zone locator**). Unincorporated → **DeKalb County Schools**. School system mismatch alone can break a comp set. (maps.apsk12.org; APS school zones.)
- **Non-comparable:** BeltLine-adjacent Ormewood premium vs deep East Atlanta; commercial Moreland/Flat Shoals frontage; floodplain near Intrenchment Creek / low Glenwood; unfinished flips vs renovated; Zone 6 crime varies block-to-block — pull APD data for the block, not zip average.
