## Area name
**Riverdale, Clayton County** — predominantly inside the **City of Riverdale** (confirm parcel; small fringe can sit in unincorporated Clayton or adjacent cities).

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**3 / 5** — Distressed-supply / BRRRR math works (~29% off for 1%), but Clayton delinquency risk, city vacant-structure registry + multifamily compliance, and elevated crime require careful underwriting.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Riverdale** Community Development / Building for parcels inside city limits; unincorporated parcels use Clayton County.
- Riverdale Code Enforcement active (citations, fines up to **$1,000** per offense per city materials).
- **Vacant / dilapidated structures:** City Chapter 18 Art. V vacant-structure registration — residential vacant registration fee cited at **$300** initial (city document); owners must secure/maintain and register.
- **Multifamily:** Riverdale ordinance amendments require code-compliance certificates for multifamily with occupational-tax renewal (inspect share of units annually; all units on a multi-year cycle — see ordinance PDF). Ordinary **1–3 unit long-term rentals:** no separate citywide SFR rental license found; Georgia § 36-74-30 blocks general rental registries.
- **Unincorporated Clayton:** enforces 2018 IPMC (as amended); ≥24-hour cure notice before misdemeanor charge except imminent danger; **no** general long-term rental registration found at county level.
- **STR (unincorporated Clayton):** owner-occupied only, business license, zoning/fire/building inspections — **not** a viable STR play on a vacant BRRRR rental.
- **Sources:** https://www.riverdalega.gov/86/Code-Enforcement ; https://library.municode.com/ga/riverdale ; https://cityrulelookup.com/counties/clayton-county-ga/rental-property ; https://www.claytoncountyga.gov/download/99/business-and-alcohol-license-forms/29667/short-term-rental-unit-license-checklist-supplemental-form.pdf

## City attitude to investors
- Code sweeps and vacant-property tools show a **maintenance-first** posture (example: Clayton County code cases after tenant complaints at large parks — Capital B Atlanta). Not an explicit anti-investor purchase ban for small LLCs.
- State law still blocks blanket rental registries; cities lean on vacant-property and IPMC enforcement instead.
- Federal 350+ SFR institutional limit (2027) — N/A to H.S HOLDINGS scale.

### Investor friendliness (South Fulton portion) — **2 / 5**
**Rationale:** Individual SFR BRRRR is still legal (GA bans local SFR rental registries), but COSF is **homeownership-protective**: vacant/foreclosed registration, multifamily inspection program, and a 2021 zoning push to **prohibit new single-family homes built for rent in subdivisions**. Airport noise, weak zoned HS (Banneker GS 2/10), and a heavier Fulton+COSF+FCS tax stack vs Clayton-only Riverdale further cut the score.

### Permits & code enforcement
- **Building permits:** City of South Fulton Community Development / Regulatory Affairs via **SagesGov** online portal (https://www.sagesgov.com/southfulton-ga). Building, trades/MEP, LDP, etc. listed on https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/2852/Building-Permits. City does **not** publish a single universal residential reno SLA; inspections requested by 4 p.m. can often be next business day (city permit pages). **CO:** obtain after required finals per permit type — exact CO checklist not restated here; follow issued permit conditions.
- **Code Enforcement:** Active division; vacant/abandoned focus; complaint + proactive tools. https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/2464/Code-Enforcement
- **Vacant / foreclosed registry:** Owners must register vacant or foreclosed real property with the city **within 30 days** of becoming vacant/foreclosed (habitable + uninhabited ≥60 days with no utility use, or incomplete without valid permit). Payment portal on city page. https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/3252/Vacant-Abandoned-Property-Request ; application PDF https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/6705/South-Fulton-GA-Vacant-Property-Application-2023
- **Multifamily (4+ units):** Annual code-compliance certificate + occupational tax; inspect 100% initially, then ≥20%/year and all units ≤5 years (Ord. 2021-018 packet). https://www.cityofsouthfultonga.gov/DocumentCenter/View/12376/MULTI-FAMILY-RENTAL-HOUSING-Information-Packet
- **SFR long-term rental registry:** **Not authorized** — O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30(b) bars local governments from requiring registration of residential rental property and limits rental inspections to probable cause.
