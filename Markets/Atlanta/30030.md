## Area name
**City of Decatur** (independent municipality inside DeKalb County). Do not confuse with “Decatur” mailing addresses in unincorporated south DeKalb (30032/30034/30035).

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**3 / 5** — Strong flip demand, walkable downtown, and top schools support ARV, but **very high city+school taxes**, expensive basis (~$600K ZHVI), and a protective local regulatory culture cut the score.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Decatur** issues building permits via online CommunityCore / permitting portal (email permittech@decaturga.com for access). Paper/email apps not accepted for STR; building permits likewise portal-driven.
- Timelines: city does not publish a hard SLA; trade permits can clear in days–~2 weeks; larger rehabs often **multiple weeks** with resubmittals (contractor blogs — soft evidence).
- Historic / design-sensitive blocks near downtown may add review — research overlay before bid.
- Code enforcement is active in a small, dense city; expect neighbor complaint responsiveness.
- **Sources:** https://www.decaturga.com/dec/page/how-obtain-permit ; https://www.decaturga.com/planning/page/short-term-rentals

## City attitude to investors
- City prioritizes neighborhood character and schools; **STR registration required beginning 2026** ($125/year, 15-day public notice, local 24/7 manager within 1 hour, annual IRC/IFC inspections, occupancy caps). Not a hospitable STR/investor-hotel market.
- No evidence of a long-term SFR rental registry (blocked by O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30). Long-term rentals still face high carrying costs.
- Politically **owner-occupant leaning**; flips that improve housing stock are tolerated, institutional bulk buying is unpopular statewide (no local ban enacted for small LLCs).
- **Sources:** https://www.decaturga.com/planning/page/short-term-rentals ; O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30

## Crime
- City of Decatur PD publishes city crime data (city-data / UCR mirrors). City proper is generally **safer than south DeKalb unincorporated tracts**, but still urban — check block groups near commercial edges.
- Zip 30030 ≠ all “Decatur” crime maps (those often blend unincorporated areas). Prefer Decatur PD / open data for the city.
- APD Zone data does **not** apply (not City of Atlanta).

## Transit & access
- Excellent for metro: MARTA **Decatur** and nearby **Avondale / East Lake** Blue Line stations; walkable downtown square; Emory / CDC / downtown Atlanta job access.
- Strong buyer pool for finished flips (walkability premium).

## Taxes
- **2026 City of Decatur millage total 33.27** (General 8.75 + Capital 2.1 + Bond 0.59 + School Bond 1.18 + DDA 0.35 + **School Fund 20.3**).
- Critical quirk: Decatur uses a **50% assessment ratio** for city bills (vs Georgia’s usual 40% for county) — effective tax burden is among metro’s highest. Example on city site for 2025: $400K appraised with homestead still ~$6.2K taxes + sanitation/stormwater.
- Investors without homestead exemptions pay full freight — **hurts hold / BRRRR exit to owner-occupants less than long holds**, but still pressures ARV comps.
- Plus DeKalb county lines on the bill for county services (city row in county presentation shows lower county millage because city provides services).
- **Sources:** https://decaturga.civicpluswebopen.com/adminservices/page/taxes-and-fees ; DeKalb 2026 millage presentation (Decatur city row 11.425 county mills)

## HOA
- Many SFH blocks have **no HOA**; townhome/condo pockets near downtown do. Condo docs can restrict rentals — review if not detached SFH.

## Insurance
- Higher dwelling limits (ARVs ~$500–700K+) ⇒ premiums above metro average even if rate/$ is similar. Decatur participates in flood CRS (city cites possible **~15% NFIP discount** for eligible policies).
- FEMA address check still required (Peachtree Creek / urban drainage).
- **Sources:** https://www.decaturga.com/dec/page/flood-protection-information ; insurance aggregates.

## Other pros/cons
**Pros (flip lens):**
- Market screen: ZHVI 3BR ~**$609.5K**; Redfin median ~**$591.8K**; SAFMR **$2,520**; ~**39%** renters; DOM ~**47**; YoY ZHVI **+1.8%**. Atlanta Market Research: ~**$76K** profit / **15.8%** ROI at 30% discount + $35/sf rehab model.
- **City Schools of Decatur** — among metro’s strongest (GreatSchools often **8–9/10** for several elementaries and Decatur High). Major ARV driver.
- Deep end-buyer demand; low months of supply (~4.0).

**Cons:**
- High capital (~$480K all-in in the 30%-off model) — concentration risk for a small LLC.
- Tax drag and strict design/STR rules.
- Competition from well-capitalized flippers; thin distressed supply.

- **Schools:** City Schools of Decatur are a primary ARV driver — do not comp to DeKalb County School District homes even one block outside the city line.
