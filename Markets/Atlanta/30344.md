## Area name
East Point, GA (metro Atlanta), ZIP 30344. Almost entirely the City of East Point (~99.07% of land area), with small slivers of College Park (~0.60%), Hapeville (~0.20%), and City of Atlanta (~0.10%). Entirely in Fulton County. **Not primarily unincorporated Fulton** — confirm municipality per parcel/address before assuming East Point permit/tax rules apply. (city-data.com/zips/30344.html, Nov 9, 2026)

Population ~36,235 (2024 est.); ~56% renter-occupied; median home value ~$311k (2024); median household income ~$70.7k; poverty ~19.7%. Housing stock is older (large share pre-1970) with some 2000s and post-2020 new build. (city-data.com/zips/30344.html, Nov 9, 2026)

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**2 / 5** — Process-heavy rehab permits, CO-on-ownership-change friction, elevated crime (improving), weak traditional public schools, and high combined millage outweigh solid airport/MARTA job access and the absence of long-term rental registration. (Nov 9, 2026)

## Permits & code enforcement
**Process (City of East Point):** Online via BS&A portal (bsaonline.com/?uid=2757). Contractors (not owner-occupants) must create a contractor account before applying; activation can take up to ~48 hours. Digital plans only (single PDF); no walk-in hard copies. (eastpointga.gov BS&A setup PDF; Plan Review Process PDF updated Dec 2025; Nov 9, 2026)

**Published plan-review turnaround (clock starts when payment is confirmed, not submit date):**
- Residential rehabs/renovations & additions: **5 business days**
- Residential new construction & all commercial: **10 business days**
- Demolitions: **14 business days**
- Paid expedited review available for one cycle (expedited fee = plan-review fee; subsequent cycles revert to standard unless paid again). (https://eastpointga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Step-5-The-Plan-Review-Process.pdf, Nov 9, 2026)

**Rehab difficulty for flippers:** Drawings/plans required for gut rehabs (sheetrock removed), floor-plan changes, new framing, new/resized openings. Residential rehab submittals commonly need existing + proposed floor plans, electrical plans + load calc, plumbing/mechanical/foundation/framing details, energy compliance, etc. Incomplete/incorrect packages are denied and must be fully resubmitted as one PDF; each resubmittal restarts the review clock. Vinyl siding called out as not allowed in plan guides. >25% siding replacement can trigger an Infill/Public Participation packet. Demolition has a long checklist (asbestos survey, utility kill confirmations, rodent letter, etc.; 10–15 business days allotted for utility confirmations before permit issues). (Plan Review Process PDF; eastpointga.gov “What Requires a Permit” PDF; Nov 9, 2026)

**Certificate of Occupancy:** City ordinance Sec. 10-3007 requires a CO in advance of occupancy/use for new buildings, alterations, change of occupancy/use, **or a change in ownership of a building, land, or business**. Commercial CO explicitly tracks new ownership of apartment complexes / landlord buildings and new business tenants. Certificate of Completion (CC) is for permitted renovations where a CO is already on file and use/SF unchanged. **Practical investor impact:** treat ownership-change CO / CO inspection as a potential closing or lease-up friction item — confirm current practice with Permits for single-family flips vs multifamily; do not assume no CO is needed after purchase. (https://eastpointga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/When-I-apply-for-Certificate-of-Occupancy-or-Completion-05052023.pdf; eastpointga.gov/permits/; Nov 9, 2026)

**Inspections:** Requested via BS&A; rough trades before framing; re-inspection fees must be paid before re-request. If city does not inspect within 48 hours of request, Georgia PE third-party reports may be used under stated rules. (Building Inspection Procedures PDF, Dec 2025; Nov 9, 2026)

**Long-term rental licensing / registration:** **No city rental registry or mandatory long-term rental license found.** Georgia law has prohibited local governments from requiring residential rental registration since 2003 (advocacy to lift the ban continues; bills have not passed as of early 2025 reporting). (https://www.wabe.org/this-legislative-session-another-push-to-remove-georgias-ban-on-rental-registries/; Nov 9, 2026)

**Short-term rentals:** Allowed with annual city permit (homestay $75; whole-house $150) + whole-house business license; 8% hotel/motel tax monthly; zoning limits apply; 65 permitted STRs citywide in 2025. No STR moratorium found. Fines $50–$1,000/day; license can be revoked after repeated code/crime issues. (https://eastpointga.gov/short-term-rentals-in-city-of-east-point/; Nov 9, 2026)

**Code enforcement intensity:** Active. 24/7 complaint hotline 404-551-3709; CodeEnforcement@EastPointCity.org. City runs blight / “50 Worst” style abatement focus; Blighted Property Task Force advises on vacant/abandoned properties. Vacant/foreclosed registration is authorized under Georgia law for municipalities (fees/penalties capped by statute) — confirm current East Point vacant-property registration practice per address. (eastpointga.gov STR page; city boards page; FY2025 budget references to 50 Worst team; Nov 9, 2026)

**Owner-note check (permits / city hard on investors):** **Partially confirmed.** Published initial review windows are not extreme, but documentation standards, resubmittal resets, rehab drawing requirements, CO-on-ownership-change language, demolition utility waits, and active blight/code posture create real time/cost risk for flips and BRRRR rehabs. (Nov 9, 2026)

## City attitude to investors
Mixed / process-heavy rather than an outright investor ban.
- No long-term rental registration (state-preempted) — favorable vs many out-of-state markets.
- STR regulated but permitted (not banned); council has discussed STR / non-owner-occupied topics (e.g. Sep 2024 work session agenda items on bank-owned housing, code enforcement, STR).
- Strong focus on blight, vacant/out-of-state-owned property, and code compliance — can feel hostile to neglected investor inventory while rewarding compliant rehabbers.
- Housing needs assessment / staff messaging emphasize neighborhood character, ADU owner-occupancy/special-use hurdles, and infill survey requirements for larger additions — not a “come flip freely” climate. (eastpointga.gov; citizenportal.ai Aug 2025 community engagement summary; Nov 9, 2026)

**No citywide flip/rental moratorium found** as of this research date. (Nov 9, 2026)

## Crime
**Direction (city UCR, East Point PD):** Improving sharply from 2024 → 2025.
- 2024 total reported crimes: **3,287** (homicides 17; agg. assault 218; robbery 94; burglary 236; MV theft 440; larceny 1,939).
- 2025 total: **2,653** (~**−19%**); homicides **10** (−41%); burglary **207**; MV theft **271** (−38%); larceny **1,518**.
- Early 2026 (Jan–Feb YTD): **424** total crimes (still early-year). (https://eastpointga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/UCR-Crimes-Report-2024_Monthly_December_rv.pdf; https://eastpointga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/UCR-Crimes-Report-2025_Monthly_December-2025.pdf; https://eastpointga.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/UCR-Crimes-Report-2026_Monthly_February.pdf; Nov 9, 2026)

Absolute rates remain elevated vs national averages on many aggregator sites (treat third-party “grades” cautiously; prefer city UCR PDFs). Property crime (esp. larceny, MV theft) dominates volume. Screen blocks carefully; crime is not uniform across 30344. (Nov 9, 2026)

**Owner-note check (“people avoid the area”):** **Partially confirmed on safety reputation, overstated as a blanket.** Crime is a real underwriting factor but trending down; rental demand and airport workforce presence argue against total avoidance. (Nov 9, 2026)

## Transit & access
**Strong relative to many south-metro zips — contradicts “transit is very bad.”**
- **MARTA rail:** East Point Station on Red & Gold lines; College Park Station nearby; Airport Station ~1–2 stops south — direct rail to Hartsfield-Jackson and downtown Atlanta. (itsmarta.com; Nov 9, 2026)
- **Bus:** Multiple MARTA bus routes serve East Point / College Park corridors (e.g. routes commonly listed for the area include 71, 78, 80, 84, 192, 193 and College Park-area routes — verify current maps). (shift-atl.com / MARTA; Nov 9, 2026)
- **Jobs:** Immediate proximity to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport employment, logistics/industrial along Sylvan/Main corridors, and MARTA-linked downtown jobs. Mean commute ~31 minutes (ACS via city-data). ~6% of workers use transit (bus/rail/streetcar combined in ACS means table); high car reliance remains. (city-data.com/zips/30344.html; Nov 9, 2026)
- Highways: I-85, I-285, Camp Creek Parkway access.

**Owner-note check (“public transit is very bad”):** **Contradicted for rail/airport access.** MARTA heavy rail + airport adjacency are among the zip’s strongest investor positives. Local bus frequency/coverage and first/last-mile gaps may still feel “bad” on some blocks — evaluate address-level. (Nov 9, 2026)

## Taxes
**Layers (investor = no homestead):** City of East Point + Fulton County + Fulton County Schools (not APS for East Point addresses). Georgia assesses at **40%** of fair market value.

**2025 all-in millage for East Point (reported):** **~39.119 mills** — City **13.000** + Fulton County component (**~9.039** on 2025 sheet) + Fulton County Schools **17.080**. East Point’s city rate is among the **highest city rates in Fulton** (only Hapeville higher on the 2025 city-rate ranking cited). (https://www.readroundabout.com/fulton-county-millage-rates/; Nov 9, 2026)

**2026 developments:**
- East Point finance recommended **rollback 13.156 mills** for 2026 (digest delay compressed timetable). Confirm final adopted rate. (https://citizenportal.ai/articles/11013610/...; Oct 13, 2026 reporting; checked Nov 9, 2026)
- Fulton County BOC kept General Fund millage **flat at 8.87 mills** for a 5th year (Oct 2026). (https://fultoncountyga.gov/News/2026/10/08/Fulton-County-Keeps-Millage-Rate-Flat-For-5th-Year; Nov 9, 2026)
- Fulton County Schools had proposed moving toward **18.080** (from 17.080) in 2026 budget discussion — **confirm final adopted school millage** before underwriting. (roundabout / roughdraft reporting; Nov 9, 2026)

**Homestead / reassessment:** Owner-occupant homestead exemptions exist (city applications + Fulton County floating homestead that can cap annual assessed-value growth for homesteaded owners at lesser of CPI or 3%). **Investor-held / LLC rentals do not get homestead protection** — full reassessment risk on purchase and annual notices from Fulton County Board of Assessors. City homestead does not apply to county/school the same way as county homestead — read each bill. Median real-estate taxes paid in 30344 reported ~$1,852 (2024, city-data) — use as rough context only; investor bills track assessed value × mills. (legalclarity.org East Point property tax overview; eastpointga.gov homestead PDF; Nov 9, 2026)

## HOA

## Other pros/cons
**Pros**
- MARTA Red/Gold + minutes to airport = tenant demand from airport/logistics workers.
- No Georgia/local long-term rental registry.
- STR path exists (if strategy includes it).
- Crime totals down materially 2024→2025.
- Older housing stock = flip/BRRRR inventory (with permit cost).
- Low HOA incidence on classic SFR.

**Cons / risks**
- Permit & plan package burden + CO-on-ownership language = schedule risk for flips.
- Active code/blight enforcement.
- Combined millage among Fulton’s highest city all-ins.
- Traditional district schools weak on GreatSchools (e.g. many elementaries 3–5/10; Woodland MS 3/10; Tri-Cities HS 1/10; some charters higher — Resurgence Hall Charter 10/10). Tenant demand may skew toward non-family / workforce. (https://www.greatschools.org/georgia/east-point/; Nov 9, 2026)
- **East Point Power** (municipal electric): documented resident complaints on outages (2024), 2025 billing-system migration that sent erroneous disconnect/autopay notices (~590 customers; city apology), and 2026 rate/seasonal pricing controversy (reports of winter ~9.3¢ → summer ~13.9¢/kWh proposals/protests — confirm current tariff). Budget for utility friction in self-managed rentals; tenants may blame landlord for shutoffs/bill errors. (11alive.com; citizenportal.ai billing-system article; eastpointga.gov apology letter PDF; wsbtv.com rate coverage; Nov 9, 2026)
- High renter share + ACS vacancy context: sources cite elevated vacancy vs tight Class-A markets (one aggregator cites ~13% vacancy from ACS 2019–2023 — treat as soft/older ACS, not a current Class-A survey). Median gross rent ~**$1,470** (city-data 2024); Zillow Rental Manager market pages have shown ~**$1,500–$1,600+** range depending on product mix — **verify live comps per beds/baths**; do not underwrite off a single scrape. (city-data; zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/30344/; Nov 9, 2026)
