# 30088 - Stone Mountain / Redan, GA (south/east DeKalb) — BRRRR secondary

## Area name
Postal name Stone Mountain; Census place is largely **Redan CDP** (~51% of land) with the rest unincorporated DeKalb. **Entirely DeKalb County.** Important: this zip is **mostly NOT inside the incorporated City of Stone Mountain** — City of Stone Mountain rules (vacation-home caps, etc.) usually do not apply; unincorporated DeKalb rules do. Confirm parcel vs city limits before relying on city vs county code. ~29k people (ACS 2020–2024).

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3 / 5** — Solid south/east DeKalb rental pocket (owner-occupied ~61% ⇒ ~39% renters), mid entry (~$239k median value), DeKalb landlord occupation tax manageable; crime and schools are the main haircuts. Better than Ellenwood on renter share; verify parcel is unincorporated DeKalb (almost always yes for 30088).

## Permits & code enforcement
- Unincorporated DeKalb: **e-Permits Portal + ProjectDox** (not Accela). Residential rehab plan review typically ~2–4 weeks for standard scopes; structural/additions longer (DeKalb Planning & Sustainability).
- Code Compliance Administration: 180 Sams St, 404-687-3700; initial inspections often within ~3 business days of complaint.
- Intensity: complaint-driven plus apartment-complex registration inspections for 4+ unit properties — active but not uniquely hostile vs other DeKalb south zips.

## City attitude to investors
- Unincorporated DeKalb — no city council. County requires:
  - **Business license / occupation tax** for rental activity (DeKalb Business & Alcohol License Guidebook 2026).
  - **Apartment Complex Registrations:** owners/managers of **4+ multifamily units** must obtain Code Compliance Certificate; properties >5 years old typically face sampling inspections (20% of units/year, all units within 5 years).
  - **STR:** unincorporated DeKalb STR license required before listing (Engage DeKalb / Deckard portal); annual renew. Not required for long-term BRRRR.
- No long-term rental moratorium found (Nov 9, 2026). City of Stone Mountain’s 60-permit vacation-home cap applies **only inside city limits** — generally irrelevant for 30088 Redan parcels.

## Crime
- Policed by **DeKalb County Police** (not City of Stone Mountain PD for most of the zip).
- DeKalb County PD 2025 summary: ~716 violent and ~3,343 property crimes per 100k (CrimeTally / agency summaries) — **county-level, not zip-level**.
- Secondary: CrimeGrade rates Redan overall **D** (violent D, property D) — **model/secondary quality; use only as flag, not underwriting number**.
- NeighborhoodScout city-of-Stone-Mountain figures are for the small city, not this zip — do not mix.
- **Gap:** pull DeKalb open crime map / Part 1 layers for the specific block group before buy.

## Transit & access
- MARTA bus routes serving Stone Mountain / Redan corridor (reported: 75, 117, 119, 121 area); **no MARTA rail in-zip**. Stone Mountain / Indian Creek rail is a drive/transfer away.
- Car-dependent commute to Emory/CDC/downtown or warehouse jobs east/south.

## Taxes
- 2026 unincorporated DeKalb county millage **21.310** + DeKalb County School District **22.78** ≈ **~44.09 mills** total county+school (before any specials). Assessed at 40% FMV.
- Non-homestead (investor) properties do not get homestead/EHOST relief — full freight + digest growth risk (DeKalb digest has risen multi-year).
- Stone Mountain city millage (~14.067 county portion for city residents per 2026 hearing table) applies only if parcel is inside city — rare for 30088.

## HOA
- Many 1980s–2000s subdivisions; HOA prevalence moderate. Check rental caps / leasing approval in covenants.

## Insurance
- DeKalb average HO ~$2.3k–$2.4k/year (2025 surveys); landlord policies higher.
- Flood: address-level FEMA MSC / Georgia DFIRM. No zip-wide special flood designation found; check creek/floodplain parcels carefully.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** Entirely **DeKalb County**, mostly **unincorporated Redan CDP** — generally **not** City of Stone Mountain.
