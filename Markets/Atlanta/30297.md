## Area name
Forest Park, Clayton County. Zip entirely in Clayton. ~65% of land in City of Forest Park; ~32% unincorporated Clayton; small other places. ~29k people (ACS 2020–2024). AMR: cheapest entry on Atlanta shortlist; ~62% renters.

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3 / 5** — Best-in-shortlist entry price + high renter share, but City of Forest Park runs a **mandatory residential rental inspection/registration program**, city millage is high (~16.7), and crime/poverty are elevated. Workable BRRRR if you budget compliance and buy well below median; not “set and forget.”

## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Forest Park** Planning & Community Development for city parcels; Clayton County for unincorporated parcels — confirm address.
- Permitting moving to **OpenGov** portal (forestparkga.portal.opengov.com); city announced new apps through OpenGov as of Oct 2026 (legacy Tyler cases still processing).
- Code Enforcement: education → inspection → notice → progressive enforcement (city planning pages).
- Difficulty: moderate; rental program adds annual paperwork/fees beyond ordinary permits.

## City attitude to investors
- **Active regulation of long-term rentals** (not just STR):
  - **Article S – Residential Rental Dwelling Unit Inspection and Maintenance Program** (Forest Park Code Title 8 Ch. 2):
    - Owners/operators must **register** for the program.
    - **Single-family rentals:** annual **self-inspection** of interior/exterior on city form; submit with annual occupation-tax application; city spot-checks.
    - **Multifamily:** annual code-compliance certificate with business-license renewal; ≥25% of units inspected/year, all units ≤4 years; exterior/common ≥50% of buildings/year.
    - New construction: interior inspection exemption for 2 years after CO (self-inspection still required).
    - Failure to register/certify → penalties, possible city inspection at **$200/unit**, liens.
  - Occupation tax applies to owners receiving rental income.
  - **STR:** separate annual license (~$150) with agent, occupancy/parking rules (city STR chapter).
- No investor purchase moratorium found. Tone is **compliance-heavy / quality-of-housing**, not anti-investor per se — still friction for self-managed BRRRR.

## Crime
- **Primary (FBI NIBRS via Police Census / Forest Park PD GA0310200):**
  - 2025: **108 violent**, **425 property** offenses (full year); pop served ~19.2k → rough rates ~563 violent / ~2,215 property per 100k.
  - Trend: violent down from 214 (2021) → 108 (2025); property down from 955 (2022) → 425 (2025). **Improving but still elevated.**
  - 2026 YTD (8 months): 71 violent, 254 property — incomplete year.
- Secondary aggregators (CrimeGrade etc.) often grade Forest Park poorly — use FBI counts above first.
- Unincorporated Clayton parcels: Clayton County PD stats (countywide) — not zip-cut.

## Transit & access
- Near Hartsfield–Jackson / warehouse corridor; I-75 / I-285 access.
- MARTA bus (e.g. route 195 area); **no MARTA rail in zip**.
- Job access: logistics, retail, airport-adjacent — supports blue-collar rental demand.

## Taxes
- **2025 City of Forest Park millage: 16.743** (unchanged several years) — among Clayton’s highest city rates.
- Clayton County unincorporated/incorporated county M&O **14.552** + school **19.600** = **34.152** (2025, GA DOR / TaxByAddress).
- **Illustrative city total ≈ 14.552 + 19.600 + 16.743 ≈ 50.9 mills** (+ fire district where applicable) — heavy vs Morrow.
- Census ACS effective rate for Forest Park city ~1.07% (higher than Clayton median 0.89%).

## Other pros/cons
**Pros**
- Median home value ~$153k (lowest of this batch); median gross rent ~$1,254; **owner-occupied only 38.2%** → strong renter share (~62%).
- Distressed / declining Redfin medians noted by AMR (−14.5% YoY on Redfin median) — buying opportunity if rehab discipline holds.

**Cons**
- Mandatory rental registration + inspection program — ongoing compliance cost.
- High combined millage inside city.
- Median HH income ~$45k; poverty ~22.7% — collection/tenant-quality risk.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Jurisdiction:** ~65% **City of Forest Park**, ~32% **unincorporated Clayton** — city rental-inspection + higher city millage apply only inside city limits. Match comps to **same city/unincorporated status**. (PlainZIP; Forest Park about page.)

Do **not** comp a 65–70+ DNL contour house to a quiet SE block without a noise adjustment. (BestNeighborhood / USDOT aviation noise layers; ATL NEM maps.)

- **Non-comparable:** Industrial/commercial adjacency on Old Dixie; multifamily vs SFR; Fort Gillem-adjacent land; floodplain parcels (FEMA/CCWA by address).
