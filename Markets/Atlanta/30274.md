## Other pros/cons

- ZHVI mid-tier 3BR ~**$192,881**; Redfin median ~**$213,937**; ZORI ~**$1,406**; SAFMR 3BR **$1,690**; ~**52%** renters; DOM ~**44**; ~**56** sales/3 mo; YoY ZHVI **−5.1%**; median year ~**1985**.
- **Rent vs SAFMR gap:** ZORI is **~$1,406 vs SAFMR $1,690 → ZORI ≈ 16.8% below SAFMR** (SAFMR/ZORI ≈ 1.20). For underwriting, prefer ZORI/comps; SAFMR mainly for HCV/Section 8.

**1% rule math (estimate):** Assumptions — purchase = ZHVI×(1−d); buy closing 2%; rehab $35/sf×1,400 = $49,000; total T = purchase×1.02+$49k; rent = SAFMR 3BR.
- Need T ≤ $169,000 → purchase ≤ ~$117,647 → **discount ≈ 39% under ZHVI** for 1% on SAFMR rent.
- If rent = ZORI $1,406 → need T ≤ $140,600 → **discount ≈ 53% under ZHVI**. Label: estimates.

**Flip test (estimate):** Sell costs 8%; need profit ≥ $30k **and** ROI ≥ 15%. Binding constraint is $30k profit → T ≤ ~$147,450 → **~50% under ZHVI**. At 30% off, model fails both hurdles (negative/near-zero profit). **Flip: fail** at normal discounts.
