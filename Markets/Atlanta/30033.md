## Area name
**North Decatur / Medlock / Toco Hills fringe**, mostly **unincorporated DeKalb County** (Atlanta/Decatur mailing addresses). Not City of Decatur (30030) and usually not City of Atlanta — confirm parcel.

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**3 / 5** — Desirable Emory-adjacent flip location with good schools and velocity (DOM ~38), but high prices and model ROI ~12% at 30% off mean you need a deeper discount or lighter rehab.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **DeKalb County** permitting (same stack as other unincorporated DeKalb). ePermits / ProjectDox; 2024 ICC since Jan 1, 2026; residential reno often ~2–4 weeks if complete.
- Code Compliance complaint-driven; multifamily 4+ CCC applies if you buy small multifamily.
- No SFR rental registry. Unincorporated DeKalb STR license if short-term.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/planning-and-sustainability/permits-plan-review-and-inspections

## City attitude to investors
- Same DeKalb county framework. North Decatur leans professional / Emory — neighbors watch construction quality and parking; expect complaint-driven inspections during noisy rehabs, not a formal anti-flip ordinance.
- No small-LLC purchase ban found.

## Crime
- Homefacts / Apartment List style summaries describe 30033 as **below national averages** for overall/violent/property crime — these are **aggregators**, not DCPD zip extracts. Treat as secondary.
- Primary: DeKalb County Police / CrimeMapping for the block. Generally stronger than south DeKalb BRRRR zips, weaker than City of Decatur core.
- **Sources:** DCPD; https://www.homefacts.com/zip-code/Georgia/Dekalb-County/Decatur/30033.html (secondary)

## Transit & access
- Strong: near Emory University, CDC, VA; MARTA bus + access toward **Avondale / Decatur** Blue Line and Emory Cliff shuttle ecosystem. Clairmont / N. Decatur / Lawrenceville Hwy corridors.
- End-buyer pool includes Emory/CDC professionals — supports flip ARV.

## Taxes
- Unincorporated DeKalb **21.310** county mills (2026) + DeKalb County School District millage + special districts.
- High ARVs (~$470K ZHVI) ⇒ large bills; non-homestead investors pay full amount; reassessment after reno material.
- **Source:** DeKalb 2026 millage presentation.

## HOA
- Mix: many 1950s–80s SFH without HOA; townhome products (e.g. Mews at North Decatur) with HOAs near Emory. Check rental rules on attached product.

## Insurance
- Scale premiums to $400–550K+ dwellings. Flood: North Fork Peachtree Creek / Peavine — FEMA check. Many interior lots Zone X.
- Georgia rate inflation applies.
