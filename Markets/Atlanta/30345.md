## Area name
**Northlake / Toco Hills / northeast DeKalb** with Atlanta mailing addresses. Mostly **unincorporated DeKalb County** (not City of Atlanta for most parcels) — confirm; a minority of parcels may touch city limits.

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**3 / 5** — Appreciating mid/high-end flip pocket with solid schools and I-85 access, but **low sales volume (~63 / 3 mo)** and high basis make execution riskier than Kirkwood.

## Permits & code enforcement
- Default: **DeKalb County** permits (ePermits / ProjectDox). Same county process as other unincorporated DeKalb zips; 2024 ICC from Jan 1, 2026; residential reno often ~2–4 weeks when complete.
- If a parcel is inside Atlanta city limits, switch to Atlanta Accela (~24-day median residential alterations).
- DeKalb Code Compliance complaint-driven; multifamily 4+ CCC in unincorporated areas.
- No SFR rental registry (state law). Unincorporated DeKalb STR license $175/year if pursuing STR.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/planning-and-sustainability/permits-plan-review-and-inspections ; https://engagedekalb.dekalbcountyga.gov/short-term-rental

## City attitude to investors
- Unincorporated DeKalb policy environment (see 30035 notes): STR licensing 2026; county interest in owner registries blocked by state law; no small-LLC buy ban.
- Northlake is more suburban/commercial — less gentrification politics than Kirkwood, more HOA/condo politics.

## Crime

## Transit & access
- I-85 / North Druid Hills / Lavista; Northlake Mall redevelopment node; MARTA **bus** (not a rail-heavy zip). Commute to Emory/CDC/Buckhead/Midtown is reasonable by car; rail requires bus transfer to Brookhaven/Chamblee or Lindbergh.
- Job access: good for Emory, CDC, Perimeter-adjacent, and northeast employment.

## Taxes
- Unincorporated DeKalb **21.310** county mills (2026) + DeKalb schools + any special districts.
- Investor non-homestead; EHOST helps owner-occupants more than LLCs.
- High ARVs (~$530K+) ⇒ large absolute tax bills after reassessment.
- **Source:** DeKalb 2026 millage presentation.

## HOA
- **High HOA / condo / townhome prevalence** around Northlake Court and similar complexes; many SFH subdivisions also have associations. Leasing restrictions common in condos — underwrite docs early. Condos can be poor flip candidates if special assessments loom.

## Insurance
- Higher dwelling limits; DeKalb average HO ~$2.3–2.5K as baseline but scale with coverage A. Flood: check FEMA — North Fork Peachtree Creek and tributaries create SFHA strips.
- **Sources:** FEMA MSC; insurance aggregates.
