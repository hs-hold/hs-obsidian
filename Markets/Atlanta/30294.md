## Area name
Ellenwood (unincorporated community spanning DeKalb, Clayton, Henry, and a sliver of Rockdale). Postal name Ellenwood; not an incorporated city. ~42k people (ACS 2020–2024). Land split approx. DeKalb 36% / Clayton 35% / Henry 27% / Rockdale 1% (Census 2020 ZIP–county file). ~92% outside any Census place; small overlap with Forest Park city limits.

**Critical:** Confirm county (and city if any) by parcel address before underwriting — permits, taxes, schools, and rental rules differ by county.

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**2.5 / 5** — Cheap-ish entry and I-675 access, but only ~19% renters (80.6% owner-occupied), multi-county jurisdiction risk, and 2025 FHFA price index down ~6.4% YoY. Better as selective BRRRR on the right county parcel than as a zip-wide thesis.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **No single city permitting office.** Work is under the county that contains the parcel:
  - Unincorporated DeKalb: e-Permits Portal + ProjectDox; typical residential rehab plan review ~2–4 weeks (DeKalb Planning & Sustainability).
  - Clayton / Henry: respective county building departments (confirm per address).
- Code enforcement likewise follows the county (DeKalb Code Compliance 404-687-3700 for DeKalb parcels).
- Difficulty: moderate once jurisdiction is known; the hard part is getting the right office.

## City attitude to investors
- Unincorporated — no city council targeting investors. Counties run occupation-tax / business-license frameworks for landlords where required (DeKalb requires a business license for rental activity; apartment complexes with 4+ units need Code Compliance Certificate).
- No zip-specific investor moratorium found (Nov 9, 2026).
- DeKalb has an active STR licensing program for unincorporated areas; long-term BRRRR is not under that STR ordinance.

## Crime
- **No reliable zip-level primary police open-data cut found for 30294** (biggest data gap).
- Policing is by county agency for the parcel (DeKalb PD / Clayton PD / Henry PD).
- County-level context (secondary quality for zip underwriting): DeKalb County PD reported elevated violent/property rates metro-wide in 2025 summaries; do not treat county rates as parcel-level.
- **Action:** pull address-level CompStat / open crime map for the specific county before offer.

## Transit & access
- I-675 and GA-42 corridor; southeast Atlanta suburb.
- MARTA bus coverage reported on routes serving the broader corridor (e.g. 74 / 195 area); **no MARTA rail station in the zip**. Car-dependent for most tenants/jobs.
- Warehousing/transportation employment cluster (ZIP Business Patterns: transportation & warehousing is the top establishment type).

## Taxes
- Assessed at 40% of FMV (GA statewide).
- **2026 DeKalb unincorporated county millage:** 21.310 mills + DeKalb County School District **22.78 mills** ≈ **~44.1 mills** county+school before city (DeKalb millage hearing PDF / school board reporting). EHOST credits apply to homestead owners; investor (non-homestead) pays full.
- **2025 Clayton (unincorporated):** county 14.552 + school 19.600 = **34.152 mills** (+ fire district ~4.146 where applicable) — TaxByAddress / GA DOR.
- **Henry:** 2025 advertised total around high-30s mills including school (confirm current year on Henry County Tax Commissioner site for the parcel).
- Reassessment risk: DeKalb digest has grown multi-year; non-homestead investors feel full millage + value growth.

## HOA
- Mixed. Newer subdivisions and some townhomes have HOAs (examples seen with annual or monthly dues); large share of older SFR may have none. Confirm covenants on title — especially any rental/lease restrictions.

## Insurance
- GA metro homeowners averages roughly ~$2.2k–$2.4k/year by county (2025 industry surveys); investor DWP / landlord policies typically higher — get quotes.
- Flood: **parcel-specific.** Check FEMA MSC / Georgia DFIRM by address. Standard HO does not cover flood. No zip-wide high-risk designation found; pockets of floodplain exist along streams in DeKalb/Clayton/Henry.
