## Area name
Postal name Lithonia, DeKalb County. **Split jurisdiction — critical.** By Census place (land share): **~57% outside any Census place** (unincorporated DeKalb), **City of Stonecrest ~27%**, **Redan CDP ~13%**, **City of Lithonia ~3%**. Stonecrest’s own FAQ: the majority of the city is in **30038**, with other parts in **30058**. A sliver is sometimes described as touching Rockdale in older city-data files; Census 2020 ZIP-county and PlainZIP treat the ZCTA as **entirely DeKalb** — confirm any east-edge parcel. ~63k people (ACS 2020–2024), the larger of Lithonia’s two residential zips. Borders 30087, 30088, 30038, 30035, 30039, 30012. Main corridors: **I-20**, **Panola Rd**, **Redan Rd**, **Covington Hwy**, Lithonia Industrial Blvd; older Lithonia city core vs newer subdivision belts.

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**3 / 5** — Solid **BRRRR** zip: large population, healthier vacancy than 30032 (~7%), decent sales volume, and a slightly higher SAFMR. DeKalb / state rules still bar an SFR rental registry. Offsets: **city-limit roulette** (Stonecrest vs Lithonia vs unincorporated), more HOA on 1990s stock, weak-to-mixed zoned schools, soft ZHVI (−4.6% YoY to Sep 2026), longer commutes, and thinner deep-rehab flip inventory than mid-century 30032. Treat as a rental-demand zip with selective flips only when the pocket, vintage, and jurisdiction line up. Do not mix 30058 comps with 30038 Stonecrest-south or City of Decatur.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **Confirm city limits on every parcel** (DeKalb GIS / tax card). Permits and code are not zip-wide.
- **Unincorporated DeKalb:** e-Permits + ProjectDox; 2024 ICC codes from Jan 1, 2026; typical residential reno review often **~2–4 weeks** when complete; Code Compliance initial inspections typically within **3 business days**; multifamily **4+** Code Compliance Certificate in unincorporated areas.
- **City of Stonecrest:** the city publishes its own **Building Department** and **CitizenServe** permit/planning portal (permits@stonecrestga.gov / 770-224-0200). Do not assume a Stonecrest address still files only with DeKalb — confirm the current portal before submitting. Stonecrest Code Enforcement is complaint-driven (city site describes reports handled on a short turnaround, often cited around 72 hours).
- **City of Lithonia:** local Building Permits page + Code Enforcement Unit (code.enforcement@lithoniacity.org / 770-482-8136 x116). Lithonia materials have indicated **no** residential rental registration (aligned with the state ban). Tiny city — do not use Lithonia city process for a Stonecrest or unincorporated house.
- **STR:** unincorporated DeKalb STR license ($175) since May 20, 2026; city STR rules may differ — check Stonecrest / Lithonia before listing.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/departments/planning-and-sustainability/permits-plan-review-and-inspections ; https://stonecrestga.gov/158/Building-Department ; https://www.stonecrestga.gov/169/Code-Enforcement ; https://www.lithoniacity.gov/1350/Building-Permits ; https://ga-lithonia.civicplus.com/1242/Code-Enforcement

## City attitude to investors
- Same state backdrop as other DeKalb files: **O.C.G.A. § 36-74-30** bars a local SFR rental registry; county officials have floated tighter owner-accountability tools (not an enacted SFR registry). No small-investor purchase ban found (Nov 12, 2026).
- **Stonecrest** and **Lithonia** add city occupational-tax / code overlays and their own political climate. Stonecrest millage is closer to the unincorporated county stack; Lithonia’s county-portion millage is lower because the city provides some services (see Taxes). Fiscal pressure is not a purchase ban, but wrong-city permitting wastes weeks.
- **Sources:** https://law.justia.com/codes/georgia/title-36/provisions-applicable-to-counties-and-municipal-corporations/chapter-74/article-2/section-36-74-30/ ; DeKalb 2026 millage presentation; city sites above.

## Crime

Vehicle theft is flagged as elevated vs the US rate. Use only as a screen; pocket variance is the point.

## Transit & access
- **I-20** is the job-access spine (downtown Atlanta, east DeKalb, Stonecrest retail). **MARTA rail is not walkable** for most of the zip; **Indian Creek** (Blue Line terminus) is the main park-and-ride hook, with buses including **Route 116 (Redan Rd)** and other east-DeKalb routes. Panola / Covington Hwy / Redan are the surface arterials.
- ACS average commute ~**38 minutes** (knowground / ACS 2020–2024) — longer than 30032 (~32). Car-dependent for most addresses. Mall at Stonecrest / Turner Hill retail is mostly the **30038** side; do not assume every 30058 address is “at the mall.”
- **Sources:** MARTA route materials; https://www.knowground.com/zip/30058 ; Stonecrest FAQ.

## Taxes
- **Unincorporated DeKalb 2026 county total: 21.310 mills**, plus **DeKalb Schools 22.78** (held for FY2027) ≈ **~44.09 mills** before specials.
- From the DeKalb 2026 millage presentation city rows (confirm on the tax bill): **Lithonia** county-portion stack shown at **14.874** mills (city provides some services); **Stonecrest** shown near **21.104** mills — **plus school millage**. City plus school plus any SSD can change cash flow; never copy an unincorporated pro forma onto a Stonecrest or Lithonia parcel.
- Non-homestead: full millage; reassessment after purchase/reno is a cash-flow risk. 40% assessment ratio.
- **Sources:** https://dekalbcountyga.gov/sites/default/files/2026-06/2026%20Millage%20Rates%20Public%20Hearing%20Presentation%202026.06.23%201.pdf ; https://roughdraftatlanta.com/2026/06/03/dekalb-school-district-millage-rate/

## HOA
- Median year built **~1995** (newer than 30032’s 1969) ⇒ **HOA / covenant prevalence is materially higher**, especially in 1990s–2000s Redan and Stonecrest-side subdivisions. Rental caps, design rules, and dues can break a BRRRR or flip budget. **Pull covenants and the resale packet before offer.** Older Lithonia city and some unincorporated pockets are less HOA-heavy — do not assume either way.

## Insurance
- DeKalb / metro HO averages ~**$2,300–$2,500/year**; landlord quotes higher. Georgia rates have been rising (AJC / industry coverage).
- **Flood:** Redfin Flood Factor (Oct 2026): **minor**, ~**2%** of 30058 properties with 30-year severe-flood risk (~521 properties). A clean knowground SFHA percentage was **not published** on the 30058 page reviewed Nov 12, 2026. Creek drainages and any Arabia Mountain / South River fringe still need **FEMA MSC by address**. Many interior subdivisions are Zone X. EPA radon **Zone 1** (county).

## Other pros/cons
**Pros**
- Scale and liquidity: ACS population **62,614**; Redfin 3 months ending Oct 2026: median sale **$259,767** / **$127 per sqft**, **162** homes sold in the reported window, 71 DOM, sale-to-list 98.5%, 35.4% price drops. Lotvox: 165 sales / 90 days through Sep 2026, 5.1 months of supply (more balanced than 30032’s ~7.2).
- Vacancy is **low vs 30032**: ACS units 23,735 and households 22,018 ⇒ ~**7.2%** vacant (BestNeighborhood matches 7.2%). Better lease-up signal for BRRRR, with the usual caveat that ACS vacancy ≠ market rental vacancy.
- ZHVI typical home **$243,564** (Sep 2026, −4.6% YoY). ZORI **$1,710**. HUD FY2027 SAFMR: 2BR **$1,650**, 3BR **$1,970** (higher voucher benchmark than 30032). ACS median gross rent $1,556; renter share of occupied units **~35%**.
- FHFA ZIP HPI: **+164%** 2015–25, **+70%** 2020–25, and **+1.1% in 2025** (unlike 30032’s −4.2% in 2025). The 2026 ZHVI decline is a separate, more recent softening.
- Established east-DeKalb rental demand along Redan / Panola / I-20.

**Cons**
- **1% rule still needs a discount.** 1% of ZHVI ≈ $2,436/mo; ZORI $1,710 is ~0.70% of typical value. SAFMR 3BR $1,970 is closer (~0.81%) but still under 1%. A 1% path on ZORI implies all-in near ~$171k.
- Multi-jurisdiction mistakes (wrong permit office, wrong millage, wrong police) waste time and money.
- Newer median vintage ⇒ fewer deep cosmetic/structural flips, more HOA product, lower sold **$/sf** than 30032’s smaller mid-century ranches.
- Schools mixed-to-weak (see Sub-areas); Arabia Mountain’s higher rating is a **theme/magnet** campus in the broader Lithonia/Stonecrest area, not a default zoned school for a random 30058 address.
- Unemployment 11.0% and poverty 18.3% (ACS 2020–2024); commute longer than inner-ring DeKalb.
- Crime: no official zip series; secondary models are poor in much of the zip; commercial corridors differ from interior subdivisions.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 12, 2026
- **Jurisdiction split (hard comps rule):** **City of Stonecrest** vs **City of Lithonia** vs **unincorporated DeKalb** (including most of **Redan CDP**). Millage, permits, and police change at the line. **Never cross city-limit lines for unadjusted comps.** Stonecrest’s core is still mostly **30038** — a 30038 Parks-of-Stonecrest / Mall-area sale is not a straight 30058 Redan or Lithonia-city comp. Confirm on DeKalb GIS / tax card. (PlainZIP; stonecrestga.gov FAQ.)
- **Favor (BRRRR, same-jurisdiction):** Interior 1990s SFR subdivisions off **Redan Rd**, **Panola Rd**, and north-of-I-20 residential streets with similar vintage and HOA status. Keep **Redan CDP**, unincorporated, Stonecrest, and Lithonia city in separate buckets.
- **Lithonia city (~3% of land):** Older, tighter lots, Lithonia PD and Lithonia permits, different millage. Not interchangeable with Stonecrest master-planned or Redan subdivision product.
- **Stonecrest pieces of 30058:** Use other **same-city** 30058 Stonecrest sales first; be cautious with 30038 south-of-I-20 / Mall at Stonecrest / Turner Hill product (retail, newer TH, different buyer pool).
- **Hard edges:** **I-20**; Panola Rd and Redan Rd as spines (same side preferred); Gwinnett / Rockdale edges on the east/north; 30035 / 30088 / 30087 school and value breaks; 30038 to the south. Commercial Covington Hwy / industrial Lithonia Industrial Blvd frontage ≠ interior subdivision.
- **Orangeburg / sewer laterals (lower zip-wide risk than 30032, not zero):** Median year built **~1995**, so most stock post-dates Orangeburg (which was generally out of use by the early 1970s as PVC took over). **Still scope pre-1980 houses**, including older **Lithonia city** fabric and any 1960s–70s pockets on the western / Redan edge. Same DeKalb rule: owner pays for the private lateral; permit a replacement. Newer subdivisions can still have clay stubs, bellies, or root issues — scope is cheap insurance, but Orangeburg is not the default assumption here the way it is in Belvedere. (BestNeighborhood ACS B25035; Foresight / Agency Atlanta sewer guides; DeKalb Watershed.)
- **Schools (all DeKalb County School District — verify the DCSD locator; several high-school clusters cut the zip):**
  - Elementaries commonly associated with the zip include **Redan, Rock Chapel, Panola Way, Princeton, Stoneview** (and others by address). GreatSchools examples in 2026 directories: several of these ES at **4/10** (Anderson-Livsey, more often a southern/Stonecrest-area school, **5/10**).
  - Middles: **Redan MS ~4/10**, **Lithonia MS ~3/10**, plus Miller Grove / Stephenson middles by address.
  - High schools: **Redan HS ~3/10**, **Lithonia HS ~2/10**, **Miller Grove HS ~2/10**, **Stephenson HS ~2/10** (Stephenson is a Stone Mountain / 30087-area campus that can pull some 30058 addresses). **Arabia Mountain HS (~7/10)** is a higher-rated **theme/academy** campus in the Lithonia/Stonecrest area — **do not assume a 30058 parcel is zoned there.**
  - School-cluster mismatch (Redan vs Lithonia vs Miller Grove vs Stephenson) is a primary non-comparability flag. Never use City of Decatur school comps. (GreatSchools Lithonia directory; zipdatamaps 30058 school list; DCSD attendance maps.)
- **Investor / SFR-fund saturation:** **No reliable zip-level corporate-ownership share found for 30058** (Nov 12, 2026). Same metro context as 30032: AJC ~**30%** of metro Atlanta SFR *rentals* investor-owned; Invitation Homes / Progress active metro-wide; DeKalb-wide figures in secondary studies are not a 30058 share. Owner-occupancy is **higher here (~65%)** than in 30032 (~56%), so the zip is less rental-dense, but 1990s subdivision streets can still have clusters of small-LLC and institutional rentals. Check Who Owns Atlanta / the tax card on the subject street. (AJC; Who-Owns-Atlanta; PlainZIP tenure.)
- **Non-comparable:** Lithonia city tiny-lot vs Redan/Stonecrest HOA subdivision; townhomes vs detached; I-20 / Covington Hwy / industrial frontage; 30038 mall-area and brand-new product; floodplain vs Zone X; theme-school (Arabia Mountain) comps for a zoned Redan/Lithonia/Miller Grove house.
