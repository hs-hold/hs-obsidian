## Area name
Smyrna, Cobb County. Zip entirely in Cobb. ~63% of land in City of Smyrna; ~33% outside Census places (unincorporated Cobb / other); small other places. ~53k people (ACS 2020–2024) — most populous of this research batch. Near Cumberland / The Battery / Truist Park job & amenity draw. AMR: most liquid flip zip on Atlanta shortlist (~201 sales), thinner ROI (~11%).

## Investor friendliness (1–5)
**4 / 5** — Clear online permits, predictable 5–10 business-day residential plan review, strong buyer demand/schools/liquidity, and investor-tolerant Cobb/Smyrna climate. Haircuts: investors **cannot** pull owner-builder permits (must use GA-licensed contractor), higher basis (~$450k median), and AMR ROI only ~11% at 30% off. Best flip ops zip in this secondary set if capital and contractor are lined up.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **City of Smyrna Building Division** (for city parcels): **all applications online only** via OpenGov — https://smyrnaga.portal.opengov.com — no in-person submittals.
- Residential plan review typically **5–10 business days**; commercial 2–3 weeks; land disturbance 4–5 weeks.
- Inspections: next business day if requested by 4 pm in portal.
- Fees (approx.): $7 / $1,000 construction cost + 10% records + $15 tech; min building permit ~$125; reinspection escalates $50→$200.
- Codes: 2024 I-codes / 2023 NEC Georgia amendments effective for apps after 12/31/2025.
- **Critical investor rule:** owner-builder permits only if property is applicant’s **current permanent residence** in legal name. Flips / landlords must hire a **Georgia-licensed contractor** to pull permits (City Building Division / local investor reporting).
- Unincorporated Cobb parcels: Cobb Community Development permitting — confirm address (many 30080 addresses are city).
- Code enforcement: standard suburban; not a known investor-hostile regime.

## City attitude to investors
- Growth-oriented (Market Village, Village Green, builder activity — Toll Brothers, Traton, etc.).
- Permit fee incentive program exists for quality development (fee reductions/waivers case-by-case).
- **STR:** annual short-term rental license + occupational tax; insurance (≥$500k liability reported), local agent, neighbor notice (CityRuleLookup / city docs). Long-term rentals: occupational tax if operating as business; **no general long-term rental inspection registry found** comparable to Forest Park.
- No flip/investor moratorium found (Nov 9, 2026).

## Crime
- Secondary city snapshot: ~2.4 violent and ~10.0 property crimes per 1,000 (2025, each.place aggregation) — **relatively favorable vs south-metro BRRRR zips; secondary quality**.
- Prefer Smyrna PD / Cobb PD open data for address-level before buy.
- Overall perception: safer flip market than East Atlanta / Clayton targets.

## Transit & access
- **No MARTA rail** in Smyrna; nearest rail ~Buckhead area (~8+ miles). CobbLinc bus limited.
- Excellent highway access (I-285 / US-41 / Concord Rd) to Cumberland employment hub — **car-oriented but strong job access**, which supports ARV/buyer demand for flips.
- Amenities (Battery, shopping, dining) help resale.

## Taxes
- **2026 Smyrna total county+city rate: 36.15 mills** = Cobb county bill rate 27.16 (8.46 general + 18.70 school; Smyrna has no county fire on this line) + Smyrna city **8.99** (Cobb Tax Commissioner 2026 Millage Rates PDF).
- Assessed at 40% FMV. City bills its own taxes separately from county.
- Lower combined millage than Forest Park city stacks; competitive for Cobb.
- Reassessment: strong values mean tax bills matter on holds; for pure flips, holding-period tax is usually secondary to ARV.

## HOA
- Common in townhome/condo/gated and many newer SFR subdivisions. Dues vary widely (examples from listings: hundreds/year to hundreds/month). **Always check rental caps / leasing approval and HOA estoppel** — can kill flip timeline or BRRRR fallback.

## Insurance
- Cobb average HO ~$2.2k–$2.3k/year (2025 surveys); renovated flips may see better rates.
- Flood: Cobb flood insurance / FEMA MSC by address; most upland Smyrna parcels low risk, but verify.
