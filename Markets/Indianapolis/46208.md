## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**2.5 / 5** — Cheap entry and rental demand exist, but **verified ~19.7% vacancy**, elevated gun-violence targeting, and sharp micro-market splits (Butler-Tarkington vs Riverside) make self-managed BRRRR high-risk without street-level screening.

## Taxes
- Mostly **Center / Washington** township mix depending on parcel — confirm on lookup. Indianapolis consolidated rates ~$2.68–$2.73/$100; **2% rental cap** typically binds. ([AribaTax](https://aribatax.com/blog/marion-county-certified-tax-rates-2026))

## Other pros/cons
**Pros:** BRT access; Butler-Tarkington flip/BRRRR upside if crime/vacancy screened; med. value ACS ~$220.5k with rent ~$1,075.
**Cons:** Verified high vacancy; concentrated gun violence; block-to-block quality cliffs; older stock rehab overruns.
**ACS 2020–2024:** vacancy **19.7%**; renters **44.1%**; med. income **$60,062**; med. value **$220,500**; med. rent **$1,075**; poverty **20.6%**.

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Do not treat as one market.** Favor comps **inside Butler–Tarkington** with other B-T sales; **Crown Hill** and **Riverside / Near Northwest** are separate buyer pools.
- **Hard boundaries:** White River / canal, **38th Street**, MLK / Northwestern Ave corridors, and cemetery/campus edges. Comps across the river or across 38th need heavy adjustment or rejection.
- **School:** IPS vs any township-district pocket — confirm school assignment; IPS vs Washington Twp MSD is a non-comparable for family-tenant rent comps.
- **Vacancy/crime:** avoid using a renovated B-T sale to support a Riverside subject (and vice versa) without explicit condition/location adjustments.
- Flood: limited SFHA share overall, but river-adjacent parcels ≠ inland B-T houses.
