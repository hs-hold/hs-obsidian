## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**4 / 5** — Soft rental registry ($5), 2% tax cap binds predictably, lower violent-crime profile than core east/northwest zips, and Decatur Twp schools (not IPS); main drag is elevated FEMA flood SFHA share and airport/industrial adjacency in parts.

## Permits & code enforcement
- **Citywide (reuse):** Residential landlords in the Consolidated City must register with DBNS; annual fee **$5** (Sec. 851-104), capped by [IC 36-1-20-5](https://codes.findlaw.com/in/title-36-local-government/in-code-sect-36-1-20-5/). Multi-property owners can register all parcels under one $5 fee. ([indy.gov landlord registry](https://www.indy.gov/activity/landlord-registration-program))
- State limits local rental inspection fees/programs under [IC 36-1-20](https://iga.in.gov/ic/2026/Title_36/Article_1/Chapter_20.pdf); complaint-based inspections still allowed.
- Building/rehab permits via DBNS **Citizen Access Portal**. Official “current review times” page last updated **Feb 2024** for residential — numerical days not extractable from text page as of Nov 9, 2026; verify live before underwriting. ([permit review times](https://www.indy.gov/activity/permit-application-review-times))
- **Continuous Vacancy Program** (effective Jan 1, 2026): after long-running unsafe/repair orders, fines can stack (~$500/90 days, up to ~$5,000/year). ~150–200 properties citywide targeted; ~60% out-of-state owners. ([WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/local/new-program-will-target-150-200-vacant-indy-homes-in-2026-continuous-vacancy-indianapolis-abandoned-properties/531-32798132-21c1-48e3-9869-6e6bb1d67fdb))
- STR: Chapter 852 permit, **$150** initial / $0 renewal, in effect since Jan 1, 2025. ([indy.gov](https://www.indy.gov/activity/landlord-registration-program))

## City attitude to investors
- No citywide cap on investor-owned single-family rentals; nearby suburbs (Fishers/Carmel) pursued rental limits, and Indiana law will block local rental caps beginning Jan 1, 2028. ([WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/money/whats-the-deal/new-indiana-law-blocks-cities-limiting-rental-homes-neighborhoods-whats-the-deal-politics/531-617c8928-a4d1-415f-bf4c-8102cc98af95))
- Advocacy pressure exists (investor ownership debates) but Indy’s practical regime remains registration + code/vacant-building enforcement, not acquisition bans. ([Mirror Indy](https://mirrorindy.org/indianapolis-documenters-housing-crisis-investor-owned-rentals/))

## Transit & access
- Near **I-70 / I-465** and Indianapolis International Airport employment; car-dependent for most trips.
- IndyGo **Blue Line BRT** (Airport ↔ downtown ↔ Cumberland via Washington St / Holt) under construction; service targeted **late 2028**. Westside construction disruption through ~2027–2028. ([IndyGo Blue Line](https://www.indygo.net/blue-line/))
- Red/Purple Lines do **not** serve this zip.

## Taxes
- **Circuit breakers:** 1% homestead / **2% other residential (rentals)** / 3% nonresidential. ([DLGF / AribaTax](https://aribatax.com/blog/indiana-rental-agricultural-property-deduction-phase-in-2026-2031))
- **New rental AV deduction** (automatic): 6% of AV for taxes payable **2026**, rising to 33.4% by 2031 (IC 6-1.1-12-47).
- Indianapolis–**Decatur** 2026 certified rate **$2.6899 / $100 AV**. At typical valuations the **2% rental cap binds**, so district rate spread inside Indy barely changes rental bills. ([AribaTax 2026 rates](https://aribatax.com/blog/marion-county-certified-tax-rates-2026))
- School corporation: **MSD Decatur Township** (Niche overall **C+**). ([Niche](https://www.niche.com/k12/d/metropolitan-school-district-of-decatur-township-in/))
- Confirm parcel township/school on Marion County property lookup — zip edges can touch Wayne Twp / other districts.
