### 1% rule (estimate) — Nov 9, 2026
**Assumptions (stated):** market rent = ZORI $1,613; rehab = **$25,000** (light–moderate BRRRR estimate); median = ZHVI $254,781.
- Max all-in for 1% = $1,613 / 0.01 = **$161,300**
- Max purchase = $161,300 − $25,000 = **$136,300** → needs **~46.5% off** ZHVI
- At **30% off** ($178,347 + $25k = $203,347): rent/cost = **0.79%** — **fails 1%**
- **Verdict:** 1% achievable only at ~**47%+ discount** to ZHVI under these assumptions (or lower rehab / higher actual street rent).

### Flip test (estimate) — Nov 9, 2026
**Assumptions:** ARV = ZHVI $254,781; purchase = 30% under ZHVI; rehab = **$35,000**; selling+holding = **10% of ARV**.
- Profit ≈ **$15,956**; ROI ≈ **7.5%** → **FAIL** both $30k and 15%
- Both thresholds first met at ~**36% off** ZHVI (est. profit ~$31k, ROI ~16%) under same rehab/sell assumptions.
- **Flip verdict:** **Fail at 30% off**; only with deeper discount or cheaper rehab/higher ARV.

### Strategy fit
