- **School-district correction (Nov 12, 2026) — do not assume Warren coverage from the zip:** Parts of 46219 are in **Indianapolis Public Schools (IPS), not MSD Warren Township.** Example: **606 Good Ave** (Irvington) is listed in a Oct 26, 2026 MIBOR/@properties listing as **Indianapolis Public Schools**, high school Arsenal Technical, even though the Census geocoder places the parcel in **Warren Township**. Township and school corporation are not always the same under Unigov. CityDesk Indianapolis (Aug 29, 2026) flags **Irvington as a known IPS / Warren split**, while most of the zip east of Irvington (Warren Park, Eastgate, and the postwar eastside tracts) is MSD Warren. **Check the school corporation on every address** (Marion County assessor taxing unit + the district enrollment office) before using school-sensitive comps or a Warren-Twp premium. The “predominantly Warren” / “stronger school district (Warren Twp)” wording above overstates coverage if it is read as zip-wide.
