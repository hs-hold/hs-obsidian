## Area name
**IMPORTANT: This file covers ONLY parcels inside the incorporated Town of Speedway.** ZIP 46224 also includes Indy/Wayne/Pike/IPS areas outside town limits — those are **out of scope** here. Speedway is an **excluded town** under Unigov with its **own** building permits, landlord license, code enforcement, **Speedway Police**, and **Speedway Schools**. Confirm town limits on every address (assessor / town parcel tools). ([Speedway gov](https://www.speedwayin.gov/); [citydesk on 46224 four-district trap](https://citydeskindianapolis.com/moving-real-estate/indianapolis-school-district-home-buyer-guide/))
