# 46201 — Near Eastside (Englewood / Little Flower / west Irvington edge), IN

## Area name
**Near Eastside** neighborhoods including **Englewood**, **Little Flower**, **Cottage Home** edge, **Holy Cross**, and western approach to **Irvington**. Peer bot label verified. ([Wikipedia Near Eastside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_neighborhoods_in_Indianapolis))

## Investor friendliness (1-5)
**2 / 5** — Deep distress pricing and rental demand, but among the metro’s highest gun-violence concentrations, ~18% vacancy, and heavy code/vacant-building risk for self-managed operators.

## Permits & code enforcement
- Citywide DBNS registry/STR/permits. Expect elevated **unsafe building / vacant** case density on Near Eastside; Continuous Vacancy Program (2026) especially relevant. ([indy.gov](https://www.indy.gov/activity/landlord-registration-program); [WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/local/new-program-will-target-150-200-vacant-indy-homes-in-2026-continuous-vacancy-indianapolis-abandoned-properties/531-32798132-21c1-48e3-9869-6e6bb1d67fdb))

## City attitude to investors
- Same citywide rules; local discourse often flags Near Eastside investor/absentee ownership — compliance and optics matter. No formal acquisition ban found.

- Proximity to downtown employment; IndyGo locals dense.

## Other pros/cons
**Pros:** Low basis; high renter share (**48.4%**); Blue Line catalyst on Washington; large unit count (liquidity for some streets).
**Cons:** Gun violence; vacancy **18.4%**; poverty **26.3%**; tenant collection / security costs; street-level comps diverge wildly.
**ACS 2020–2024:** vacancy **18.4%**; renters **48.4%**; med. income **$49,195**; med. value **$170,100**; med. rent **$941**; poverty **26.3%**.
