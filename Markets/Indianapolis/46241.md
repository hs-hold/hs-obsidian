### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Favor:** Mars Hill residential grid comps with like condition/vintage; separate **West Indianapolis / The Valley** and **Stout Field** clusters.
- **Do not cross:** Indianapolis International Airport / Ameriplex industrial into residential without discarding; **I-70** and major rail as hard barriers.
- **Township/school:** Stay inside **Wayne Twp / MSD Wayne**; never use Speedway town comps for Indy–Wayne subjects (different rate + schools). ([AribaTax](https://aribatax.com/blog/marion-county-9-townships-property-tax-differences))
