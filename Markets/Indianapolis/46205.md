## Taxes

### Sub-areas & boundaries (for comps) — Nov 9, 2026
- **Never blend** Meridian–Kessler comps into Mapleton–Fall Creek subjects (or Fairgrounds / Keystone apartment corridors) without treating as different markets.
- **Hard boundaries:** **Fall Creek**, White River, **38th Street**, College Ave / Meridian corridors, Monon Trail commercial strip.
