## How the partners want the territory
- Keep lists of target areas and zips, and build a local contact list (agents, contractors, wholesalers, property managers, hard-money and DSCR lenders, title / closing attorney, insurance, inspectors, a local bank, and investor groups). Prefer people who cover as many of the relevant zips as possible (owner, 2026-11-16).
- Only contiguous areas that local contacts typically cover; exclude isolated zips that most people they meet probably do not work in (owner, 2026-11-16).
- Purchase budget is larger than $150k; evaluate up to about $300k if returns still meet criteria, including $250/mo BRRRR cash flow (owner, 2026-11-16).
- Indianapolis Market Research (2026-11-16) found that at market rents, BRRRR cash flow of $250/mo often stops around ~$150k in core zips, and can stretch toward ~$200k only with 4-bedroom houses at HUD voucher-level rents. Flip still works at higher prices when resale is about 1.65× purchase. Use this as a market pattern, not a hard cap — every deal is still analyzed on its own numbers.

## Basement and above-grade area (owner, 2026-11-19)
- ARV uses above-grade GLA only. A finished basement adds exactly 50% of above-grade $/sf × finished sf as a separate line. Unfinished basement and unfinished upper floors add $0. This applies to the site prescreen and Deal Analysis.
- The listing API has a single sqft field with no above-grade split. The Marion County Assessor has the split for free. Research: `/home/box/shared/research/indy-basement-sqft-2026-11-19.md`.
- Comp boundaries: comps don't cross I-65, I-70, I-465, I-69, rail, N Meridian, Madison Ave or 38th St, and don't mix Lawrence with Indianapolis. Radius 0.5 mi.
