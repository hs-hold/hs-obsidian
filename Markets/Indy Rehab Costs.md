# Indy Rehab Costs (component research, 24.11.2026)

Related: [[Indianapolis]] · [[Criteria Map]] · full table: `/home/box/shared/indy-rehab-costs/indy-rehab-costs.md` (+ `.csv`)

Owner request via Grok Bot (call 24.11 13:07). Research only. Not a rule change; the rehab defaults stay as they are until the owner approves.

## Key ranges (installed, Indianapolis unless marked)
- **Windows:**
  - Vinyl insert, investor grade: $475–850 per window, mid ~$650 (est.).
  - Mid-grade: $600–1,100.
  - Angi whole-house average: $7,357.
- **Paint and floors:**
  - Interior paint: $2–6/sf of floor area. Sungate investor price is $3.20–3.50.
  - LVP: $4–9/sf, mid ~$6. Carpet: $2.60–6.50/sf.
- **Popcorn, doors, lighting:**
  - Popcorn removal: $1–2/sf scrape only, $2.50–4.50/sf finished.
  - Prehung interior door: $375–1,035, mid $545.
  - Light fixture: $70–240.
- **Kitchen** (mostly national sources):
  - Budget/rental turn: $5,850–14,100 (national).
  - New stock cabinets: $10k–16k (Indy, Hoosiers GC).
  - Appliances: $1.8k–4k (national).
  - Quartz: $50–82/sf. Laminate: $11–38/sf.
- **Bath:**
  - Refresh: $3.5k–7.5k.
  - Full remodel: Angi average $10,172. Investor GC figure is ~$4k–7k per bath.
- **Big 5:**
  - Roof: $450–750 per square.
  - HVAC full: $8.5k–18k.
  - Rewire, 1,000–1,500 sf: $10k–16k, plus panel ($2.5k–4.2k).
  - Repipe: $4k–15k.
  - Sewer: $4.5k–18k.
  - Water heater: $1.1k–2.2k.
  - All internal Big 5+1 defaults fall inside these ranges.

## Verdict on the $25–35/sf tiers (1,200 sf 3/1 sample, mid prices, +10% contingency)
- Light refresh comes to ~$18.5/sf, which **supports** the under-$25 tier.
- Adding popcorn and doors comes to ~$25.9/sf, which **supports** the $25 tier.
- Adding a kitchen comes to ~$36–38/sf. That **contradicts** the $30 tier and runs just above the $35 cap. About $31/sf is reachable only with a cheap rental-turn kitchen (~$6k).
- Hoosiers GC's figures: cosmetic $15–25/sf, moderate (with kitchen/bath) $35–55/sf.
